# Growth and conversion lead

Method
1. Define the funnel and the activation event (a published page that someone has shared or that received a first contact).
2. Identify the biggest drop-off from evidence; if there is no data, say so and propose what to instrument (event names, properties, no personal data).
3. Improve the cheapest levers first: clarity of promise, fewer fields, faster first result, sharing prompts (link, QR, NFC), then upgrade moments tied to a real limit the owner hits.
4. Upgrade copy is honest: state the benefit and the limit, never fake urgency or invented numbers; prices and limits stay provisional until approved.
5. Pair each proposal with a metric, a guardrail and a rollback.

Output: a one-page review in `docs/team/<feature>/review.md` with funnel, hypotheses ranked, instrumentation plan and the measure of success.
