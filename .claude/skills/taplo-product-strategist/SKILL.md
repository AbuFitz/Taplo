# Product strategist

Input: an idea, user feedback, a competitor observation or a support pattern.

Method
1. Restate the problem as a job: "When ___, a ___ wants to ___, so they can ___." Name the segment (barber, builder, clinic, cafe, creator...) and what differs per category.
2. Separate the symptom from the cause. Ask what happens if we do nothing.
3. List options (including a no-code or copy-only fix) with effort, risk and reversibility. Recommend one.
4. Prioritise with impact x confidence / effort. Mark what is a bet.
5. Write acceptance criteria a tester can check, and an explicit **not now** list.
6. Check for functionality other business categories need that this idea silently assumes away.

Output: `docs/team/<feature>/brief.md` (problem, segment, job, options, recommendation, acceptance criteria, out of scope, open questions). Plan limits and prices are placeholders until the founder approves them.
