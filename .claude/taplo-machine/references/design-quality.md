# Taplo visual and UX quality

Read the shared fundamentals and only the section for the current surface. A rubric directs attention; it does not replace seeing the actual result.

## Shared fundamentals

**Content earns the composition.** Establish identity, offer and next action before adding ornament. Compare alternatives using the same realistic content. Use the business's actual asset availability; a no-image state must be a complete composition, not an empty photo template.

**Hierarchy has a clear reading order.** At the actual viewport, identify first, second and third attention points. Avoid giving every row equal card weight or hiding key information in a dozen subtle labels. Dense can be calm when alignment, grouping and emphasis are deliberate.

**Type is a system with purpose.** Respect approved fonts. Set hierarchy using size, weight, line length, leading and space, not a different style for every label. Inspect 1-, 2- and 4-line business names and realistic long service names. Do not apply an arbitrary 7× display/body ratio to an operational dashboard.

**Colour is semantic and contextual.** Distinguish page, surface, primary/secondary text, border, action, selected, focus, error and disabled roles. A beautiful opaque-pair ratio does not verify gradient edges, translucent layers or text over images. Selection needs a non-colour signal. Preserve chosen colours on layout changes and offer reset explicitly.

**Components coordinate without becoming identical boxes.** Service rows, bookings, reviews and product shelves have different information density and action needs. The page should feel authored as a whole; do not force every widget into the same bordered-card wrapper.

**Control is visible.** Preview changes appear where the user can see them. Cropping, style application and destructive/derived asset changes remain reversible. Buttons say what happens. Focus and errors remain reachable when keyboard, sticky footer and browser chrome consume height.

**Motion explains a change.** The still result must work first. Use action-driven transitions for selection, expansion and saving; keep interruption and reduced-motion paths correct. Autonomous drift, every-section fade-up and oversized zooming require a specific benefit and owner compatibility. A poorly composed screen does not become desirable by moving.

**Assets belong to the concept.** Real product captures can be the strongest product visual. Generated raster, SVG, photography and animation are different media, chosen for a job. Maintain crop, light, texture and style consistency; remove generic assets that weaken specificity.

## Marketing

Visitor job: understand what Taplo makes possible, believe the evidence and choose an appropriate next action.

- Open with a characteristic product moment, not a generic dashboard floating above an empty gradient.
- Let real business-page variety demonstrate capability. Each example should reveal a distinct content/composition possibility with readable detail.
- Build progression: result → how it happens → useful capabilities → trust/commercial clarity → action. Each section contributes new information.
- Demonstrate one interaction well rather than scattering unrelated autoplay scenes.
- Use typography, pacing and contrast to support the narrative. Expressive scale is welcome where reading and navigation remain clear.
- Keep promises consistent with operational product capability. Do not create customer counts, testimonials, feature availability or “AI” claims for decoration.

Material failure: impressive motion or art obscures the actual product, repeated feature sections say the same thing, or the primary action lacks context.

## Public business page

Visitor job: recognise the business, judge suitability and take the intended booking/contact/purchase action.

- Identity, offer and action are clear in the first useful viewport; the business owns the visual identity, Taplo chrome recedes.
- Short and long names, absent logo, absent cover and both assets feel intentional. Cover crops support the subject; logos retain aspect and sufficient contrast.
- Services show the useful comparison data: name, price/duration where applicable, and action. Use row/grid/editorial variants according to content, not arbitrary preset names.
- Trust content is truthful and legible. Reviews are readable evidence rather than a decorative star strip.
- Bookings/contact affordances have clear consequences; a visitor should know whether an action opens a form, external booking provider, phone or messaging app.
- Layout diversity is structural: hierarchy, imagery, section relation and density. A recoloured stack is not a new composition.
- At 390px, the whole page should feel native to that width, not a miniature desktop design. At desktop width, avoid stretching a mobile card into dead space.

Material failure: gallery thumbnail promises a layout the renderer cannot create; needed actions are hidden; example facts masquerade as a real business; missing images create broken gaps.

## Onboarding

Visitor job: see their business coming to life, make a few understandable choices and retain a useful draft.

- Name and selected appearance visibly change the actual preview. Preview and controls remain connected throughout the flow.
- The movable mobile sheet has purposeful states. Partial preview communicates the result; full preview is accessible; keyboard mode temporarily prioritises input and then restores composition.
- Looks/Colours offer coherent choices and clear overrides. Give thumbnails enough room to judge. Keep a visible selected state and avoid stacked technical decision menus.
- Recommendations use actual content/assets and remain suggestions. Category does not become an industry design lock.
- Optional content is honestly optional. Samples are labelled and removable. Never invent actual availability or services for a real owner.
- Plan comparison is concise, uses verified entitlements and includes Free. Distinguish preference, account creation, successful save and paid subscription.
- End with the agreed account/draft/Studio sequence. No fake progress, forced wait or premature success claims.
- Back, refresh, resume and save failure preserve the owner's work. Progress indicates position without pretending all skipped details are complete.

Material failure: form content clipped by headings or sticky actions; preview vanishes unexpectedly; tiny look cards; silent palette replacement; account-first competitor imitation; successful-looking but unsaved draft.

## Studio

Visitor job: understand page status, make changes, handle relevant business work and control publication/sharing.

- First-run and established-business states are separately authored. The former foregrounds the actual draft and a useful next action; the latter prioritises tasks needing attention.
- Page state, save state and publication state are easy to distinguish. “View page” does not ambiguously mean editing, draft preview or public output.
- Navigation uses the owner's mental model and reveals current location. Avoid gratuitous modes and multiple names for the same task.
- A checklist reports real omissions; an entered name is not incomplete because an optional logo is missing. Permit useful tasks in any order.
- Empty metrics do not dominate or imply failure. Sample mode, no activity, loading, error and no permission are different states.
- The editor keeps structure, preview and inspector related. Mobile edit/preview switching retains the current task and focus; desktop panes earn their space.
- Upsells are contextual and secondary. A first-run owner should not have to negotiate decorative hero cards and upgrade prompts to edit their service.

Material failure: a greeting and dark hero replace information architecture; duplicated setup prompts compete; huge phone leaves no working space; metrics claim live integrations or activity that do not exist.

## Inspiration gallery

Visitor job: recognise a suitable starting point, understand its visual character and apply it without losing content.

- Curate distinct, complete businesses using the real renderer. Consistent capture framing makes differences understandable.
- One coherent filter model; search and visual controls have clear roles. Do not let filters become the page's main visual.
- Preview enough content to understand the design beyond a hero crop. Make selected filters and empty-result recovery clear.
- “Use this design” preserves content, assets and deliberate overrides, with a review/undo path. Explain what changes using user-facing language.
- Category is a useful suggestion, not a locked design inventory. Thumbnail, preview and actual output must agree.

Material failure: huge counts of low-quality recolours, unreadable phone thumbnails, mismatched gallery/public renderer, or loss of user content when applying a look.

## Review disposition

Tag each finding with capture/state, specific region, consequence and corrective action. Separate direction problems from execution problems. Blocking/material findings require revision; small polish can be recorded honestly. Missing current visual evidence means unable-to-assess, not a provisional pass.

Use numerical scores only as explicitly subjective review aids, with evidence. Do not set a magical score threshold that certifies Apple quality, competitor superiority, conversion or real-user preference. Preserve the owner's final judgement while completing routine work autonomously.
