# Direction brief (product strategist, from the founder's inputs)

Written from the founder's feedback across the build. Where a line is inferred it says so.

## Who and what
Taplo gives local service businesses (barbers, builders, trades, clinics, cafes, tutors, coaches, dealers) a page that wins customers: services, prices, booking, contact, products, sharing by link, QR or NFC. Self-service: no need to talk to us to get set up or to use it.

## The feel (stated by the founder)
- Down to earth and business-like. Not decorative, not playful for its own sake. Looks come from the original page finishes (Warm sand, Paper, Ink, Mono flow, Midnight flow, Graphite silk) plus a few diverse additions; most are solid and calm, a few have a subtle moving gradient.
- Monochrome and frameless: restrained palette, few borders, no boxed-in clutter, curved rather than square corners (pill buttons by default, owners can change them in the dashboard).
- Minimal text. No repeated or explanatory subtext where the control is obvious. Names are short and grey, not bold black.
- Every screen consistent: one system of spacing, radius, shadow and type so it feels polished, closer to how Linktree, Notion and Canva feel.
- Mobile is a release requirement, not a responsive afterthought (390 px, keyboard open, safe areas).

## Outcomes wanted
1. Fewer "this is not working / how do I use this" messages: the product explains itself with good defaults, honest empty states, short feature illustrations and clear next actions.
2. Every business category finds what it needs (inferred: bookings for barbers, enquiries and service areas for trades, stock for dealers, menus for cafes, packages for coaches).
3. Setup reaches a good-looking page fast, recommends sensible choices (look, arrangement, goals) from what we know, and never traps the owner.
4. Marketing shows the product truthfully: real pages, short loops of real features, no invented numbers, testimonials or customers.

## Constraints
- Plan prices and limits are provisional placeholders until approved. No payments, email or accounts are connected, and copy must say so plainly.
- No generated human portraits. Third-party assets need a recorded licence.
- No public deploy or merge without the founder's approval.

## Not now
Real analytics collection, payments, email, accounts, native apps, custom illustration produced in desktop-only editors.
