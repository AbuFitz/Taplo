# Linktree flow, from the owner's screen recording (2026-10-29)

**How to use this: immersion, not a template.** The owner recorded it so the team understands what a very good product feels like to use: pace, detail, the quality of each moment. Do not copy its structure, screen order, layouts, wording or visuals. There is more than one good way to do each of these things, and Taplo should find its own.


Source: the owner's recording of a real Linktree account, 5m11s, desktop 1664x816. Key frames are in `.claude/refs/linktree-recording/` (ignored by git, other company's product). Observed by sampling a frame every 10 seconds; no audio or interaction timing was analysed.

## Onboarding, in order (t = seconds into the recording)
1. t=70 Plan page ("Start a free trial", payment) reached from an Upgrade button.
2. t=90 **Choose a username**: one field with prefix `linktr.ee/`, three suggested available alternatives under it, a bot check, a disabled Continue. A full-bleed coloured illustration panel fills the right half.
3. t=100 **Claim a free 7-day Pro trial**: a three-point timeline (Today, In 5 days, In 7 days), Skip top right, one purple button.
4. t=110 **Which best describes your goal?** Three large cards (Creator, Business, Personal), each with a one-line promise and a colourful illustrated tile; selected card has a black outline. A thin segmented progress bar sits at the top.
5. t=120 **Add your links**: "Your selections" (for example WhatsApp phone number) then three plain URL fields; Skip and Continue.
6. t=130 **Upload profile image**: crop modal with Delete and Crop.
7. t=140 **Let's apply some colour**: a full-screen wash in the chosen colour, the phone with the profile and three buttons centred, and a bottom sheet with Wallpaper and Buttons tabs and ten colour swatches; one black Continue.
8. t=150 "Looking good. Let's keep building…" a short transition, then the admin.

## Admin (t=160 onwards)
- Left icon rail: Content, Header, Design, Enhance. Editing panel left, live phone right with Share and Copy link.
- Content: Links and Shop tabs, "Add collection" and a black "+ Add"; "Linktree tip" cards with a one-tap tick or dismiss; rows with a three-dot menu.
- Add dialog (t=20): search or paste a link, categories on the left (Suggested, Commerce, Social, Media, Contact, Events, Text), each option with an icon, a name and one line.
- Header (t=190-230): five layout thumbnails (Classic, Hero, Banner, Cutout, Shape), profile image, banner image (upload, video, Canva), title, bio.
- Design (t=40-60, 260): Theme grid of named large thumbnails with a Shuffle button; rows for Wallpaper, Buttons, Text, Colours, Stickers, Footer, each opening a sub-panel with Cancel, Save, undo and redo. Buttons: style (Solid, Glass, Outline), corner roundness, shadow, colours. Wallpaper: Fill, Gradient, Blur, Pattern, Image, Video.
- Paid features are previewable; saving one opens an "Upgrade to Pro?" dialog.
- Setup checklist (t=10, 290): a ring with "1 of 6 complete"; items: add name and bio, add profile image, add socials, add 3 links, customise your design, share.
- "Tools to grow your audience" (t=290): cards with real photography or illustration, a title, a line and one button.
- Bookings (t=300): a dialog with a calendar illustration, "Make more sales by making it easy", Continue; a warning row "Connect a calendar".

## Principles worth understanding (not instructions to copy)
- The delight is one **colour moment** with the live page, not a long form. Taplo's equivalent: the stage behind the phone takes the chosen look's colour while the sheet stays monochrome.
- Each onboarding screen has one job, big hit targets and, where it helps, an illustrated card. Taplo uses photographic category cards (real generated photography) in place of illustration.
- Account and plan come early in Linktree. Taplo keeps account last (locked owner decision); the comparison is noted, not copied.
- The admin tells the owner what to do next with a ring checklist and tip cards. Taplo's Studio home already leads with one next action; tip cards with photography are a candidate.
- Editing panels carry Cancel, Save, undo and redo at the top. Taplo's Design controls have undo and reset; a consistent header is a candidate.
