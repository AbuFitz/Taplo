# Linktree setup flow, as documented

Sources: Linktree Help Center, "How to create your first Linktree" (https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/5434134-create-first-linktree, read via TinyFish); and captions of 2022 screenshots on nicelydone.club (https://nicelydone.club/examples/apps/linktree). The images themselves could not be downloaded from the sandbox, so what follows comes from text only. The 2022 captions may be out of date.

Order, current docs: email, username, plan, then "follow the prompts to add your first links and pick a theme".
Order, 2022 captions: claim username with email and password (inline "already taken" error), name, plan choice (Free, Starter, Pro, Premium), optional checkout, then the editor with an empty "add your first link" state beside a live mobile preview.

What this means for Taplo:
- Linktree captures the account first, not last. An earlier critic run proposed account last; that was a guess and is withdrawn.
- Its form is short because the editor does the setup. The first link, theme and details are done in the editor next to a live preview, not in a long form.
- The username is claimed early and validated inline.
- Plan choice sits before the editor and can be free, so it is not a barrier.
- Still to establish with the user: the exact prompts between plan and editor, and what Linktree asks about goals or category today.
