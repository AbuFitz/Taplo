# Taplo product and design contract

Reconciled 2026-10-29 from `docs/product-decisions.md`, `docs/design-spec.md`, the repository instructions and the owner's briefs. The latest explicit owner instruction wins over older briefs and skill defaults.

## Settled (do not change without an explicit owner instruction)
- Account creation at the end of onboarding. Plan comparison near the end, Free clearly available.
- Live preview behind the movable mobile sheet, with accessible full-preview and return controls.
- One renderer, one draft state. No second preview renderer.
- Business content and images are optional. Typography-led designs are complete without images.
- Categories suggest content; they never restrict design access.
- Manual design and colour choices survive later changes. Existing customer designs stay compatible.
- Completion creates a draft. Publishing is a separate action. No public deploy or merge without approval.
- Looks/Colours control and agreed titles stay. Plan prices and limits are provisional until approved.
- No invented reviews, ratings, prices, awards or availability. Sample content is labelled.
- No generated human portraits. Monochrome product UI; colour belongs to imagery and marketing.

## Current scope
One complete path: create a draft, choose its appearance, enter Studio, edit it, inspect the customer page. Then three complete sample businesses (barber/beauty, mobile detailing, tutor/coach), the eight approved colour directions on real components, remaining widgets and overlays, then marketing, navigation and pricing.

Boundary: the earlier colour-only task did not authorise widget reconstruction. Structural variants live in an isolated playground or new variant; existing customer layouts are not rewritten silently.
