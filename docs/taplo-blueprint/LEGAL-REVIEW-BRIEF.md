# Taplo · Concrete legal review brief

Edition 2.4: ten substantive drafts now exist in legal/, with a reviewed-schedule/publication gate in LEGAL-PACK-INDEX. These are not approved terms. Use actual latest provider/payment decisions; references below to unapproved arrangements describe their original evidence state.

Edition 1.0 · UK-first proposed platform. Use with specs 74–76, the source ledger and actual product/funds/data diagrams. This is a work brief for qualified review, not final terms.

## Product facts to verify

Taplo intends to provide merchant mini-spaces, native goods/services/class transactions, discovery, customer accounts/activity/messages, staff workspaces, shareable professional profiles, rewards/Wallet, custom domains and Taplo-sold NFC accessories. Exact launch activities, geography, entity and payment model are unapproved. User-facing workflows and operational roles are in the pack. Do not draft legal text as if every intended capability launches together.

## Questions requiring a recorded answer

1. Identify the contracting entity for merchant software, customer platform service, merchant supply and Taplo hardware. Confirm public business details, governing-law/jurisdiction/consumer-rights handling and authority to enter the contracts.
2. Confirm legal seller in each reference journey: shop employee, independent chair renter, tutor company, maker and workshop. Identify receipt/support disclosures and any agency/venue relationship; employment/tax status cannot be decided by a software toggle.
3. Review the proposed funds flow and provider agreement. Who holds/controls money, bears negative balances/refunds/disputes and can initiate adjustments? Does the actual model create payment-services or stored-value obligations? Review rewards/gift-card possibilities separately before enabling them.
4. Determine consumer pre-contract, cancellation, early-service/digital-supply consent and dated-event/personalised/perishable exceptions for the exact supported offers. Review fair no-show/deposit terms and whether the charging mechanism is properly authorised. Never approve one blanket “no refunds” setting.
5. Resolve current enacted subscription rules, commencement/transitional applicability and distinction between merchant B2B SaaS and customer B2C membership. Specify notices, renewal/cooling-off/cancellation and version/evidence requirements.
6. Assess platform reporting/tax/VAT scope and seller due diligence with a qualified accountant/tax adviser. Specify required data, lawful purpose, reporting/correction/export and seller notifications where applicable.
7. Allocate controller/processor roles by processing purpose. Review DPA, privacy notices, subprocessors/international transfers, lawful bases, sensitive data, incident/rights processes and retention/deletion/backup treatment.
8. Assess actual child access and relevant Children's Code/safety duties. Separate guardian/payer authority, learner access, support information, messaging, geolocation and public portfolio consent.
9. Assess reviews/messages/content/discovery under relevant online-safety/consumer rules, including moderation/report/appeal processes and responsibility. Do not assume internal search alone decides classification.
10. Review marketing/cookies/SDKs: each sender, channel, consent/soft-opt-in conditions, suppression and transactional-message boundary. No platform-wide consent inheritance from a merchant purchase.
11. Confirm rights/licences for code, fonts, art, third-party imagery, merchant/staff portfolios, trademarks and customer photos. Review employment/contractor IP and post-departure media/data rights. No brand clearance has been established in this pack.
12. Review Taplo accessory product roles, supplier conformity/traceability/returns/recall and the promised duration/conditions of managed NFC redirects. Determine what happens after SaaS cancellation before selling a physical item with an ongoing digital promise.
13. Review registrar/reseller/registrant arrangements, domain order/renewal/refund/transfer rights, DNS/email risk and merchant exit. Ensure Taplo terms do not imply ownership it does not have.
14. Review acceptable-use/supported-category rules, suspension/appeal, complaints/escalation/ADR information where applicable, liability/indemnities/insurance and service support claims against actual capabilities/staffing.
