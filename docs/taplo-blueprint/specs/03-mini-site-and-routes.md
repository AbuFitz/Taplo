# One mini-site with context-aware entry

Current continuation · 2.6: access, repository/domain, native release, rewards/Wallet/widget/scanner and launch decisions are consolidated in the new root documents linked from START-HERE. These resolve earlier open method/binary/domain questions; actual implementation/provider/legal evidence remains required.

Status: recommended interaction contract. Depends on: 01, 02, 04.

The public experience is a compact, typed block/widget composition with a business-owned identity. Product catalogues browse horizontally on the landing page. Services use an appropriate compact list, tile or shelf. Details, media, cart and booking/request steps open in coherent popups/sheets. Owners choose blocks, variants, emphasis and order within coordinated design families. This should read as a distinctive mini-space rather than a traditional multi-section website. Larger collections can open a deliberate collection surface while the public block stays bounded. See specs 31–34 for the current visual and interaction contract.

Proposed routes, for discussion: `/b/{handle}`, `/b/{handle}/offers/{offer-id}`, `/b/{handle}/book/{offer-id}`; private activity under `/me/...`. Reserve system names. Public handles are aliases; stable IDs underpin links and ownership. A slug change redirects old public links. Never expose private record access merely through an unguessable slug.

The same route can present an item as a sheet when opened from the business page and as a complete item screen when opened directly. Browser Back closes the contextual detail and restores position. A shared item link opens the correct item without needing a previous page visit.

Store layout and brand separately from offer facts. A new theme cannot silently alter a service's price, availability or policies. A marketplace result cannot use a cheaper historical price to attract clicks to a now different offer.

Acceptance: reload an open item, open its link in another browser, press Back, follow an old handle and switch entry route; all resolve coherently. Deleted items show a useful unavailable state with a business link rather than a false purchasable offer. Private receipts survive publication changes with appropriate access.
