# Prove the connected experience and price it afterwards

Status: recommended business discovery plan.

The vision can be broad; the first proof should be narrow enough to observe. Working priority updated in edition 1.6: use an independent repeat-service operator (barber/small service-plus-retail reference) for the first end-to-end packet, with mobile quote and maker/workshop scenarios as comparators. This is a validation focus, not a final launch restriction or established demand. Interview actual owners about recent work and watch tasks; preference for a pretty page does not establish willingness to pay.

Benchmark current products using equivalent tasks: first offer setup, adding a second capability, editing information once, completing a mixed journey where supported, handling a cancellation/refund, and returning as a customer. Record completion, elapsed active work, errors, requests for help and retained external tools. Study current workflows, plans and geography, not old product stereotypes.

Sequence by complete journeys: mini-site and offer truth → one reliable transaction/fulfilment path → owner Today and customer Activity → connected second capability → repeat use/rewards → discovery expansion. Permissions, financial integrity and support recovery run through every stage. No phase is approved merely because screens exist.

Build economics from processor/Connect costs, messaging, storage, shipping/hardware fulfilment where relevant, support time, fraud losses, onboarding and acquisition. Compare total useful cost against alternatives. No plan names, prices, unlimited allowances or zero-commission promises are committed.

Acceptance: an unrelated owner publishes a correct offer without coaching; a customer completes it and finds the receipt/next step unaided; the owner resolves a realistic exception. Decide proceed/change/stop from evidence. A polished prototype and founder enthusiasm are not substitutes for those outcomes.
