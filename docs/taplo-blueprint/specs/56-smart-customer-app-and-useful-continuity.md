# 56 · Smart customer app and useful continuity

Edition 0.9 · A smart experience uses trustworthy state and appropriate shortcuts before AI. These are proposed behaviours, not claims of unique inventions.

## A useful return, without another feed

Keep Explore, Saved, Activity and Account as the current main destinations. Do not add Home, Discover and Feed merely to distribute empty content. Recommend an optional compact “Next up” area on the relevant return surface and a consistent Activity attention section. Its contents come from actual customer commitments. A deep link always opens its intended record before optional suggestions. Users can dismiss non-essential reminders; financial/policy requirements remain findable even when a reminder is dismissed.

Priority recommendation: safety/security or time-sensitive service issue; customer action with real deadline; imminent booking/collection; ongoing work; optional repeat suggestions. Explain the reason in plain words: “Your quote expires tomorrow”, “Ready for collection”, “Finish choosing a time”. No artificial urgency, fake activity badges or inferred deadlines. Deduplicate notices that reference the same record; one concise item can link to details. Do not use unread dots for content merely because the user has not browsed it.

## Behaviours and guardrails

| Useful behaviour | Source of truth | Customer experience | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Return to a business | Saved/recent permitted relationship | Fast route to the same mini-space | Visiting/buying does not imply following or marketing consent |
| Continue a task | Permitted task draft and current version | Resume the relevant step | Revalidate stock/time/terms; do not replay payment or submission |
| Repeat booking | Prior service plus current eligibility | Suggest service/staff with change option | New time, price and policy require review |
| Reorder | Previous items and current catalogue | Preselect available equivalents | No silent substitution, old-price promise or duplicate order |
| Collection with appointment | Same-merchant fulfilment and appointment rules | Offer an eligible collection choice | Requires inventory/time agreement; no assumed reservation |
| Location-aware browsing | Explicit area/permission and coverage rules | Show relevant local/mobile options | No private address exposure or fake precision |
| Relevant offer windows | Query and published offer capabilities | Match product/service/class intent | Owner category cannot hide valid additional capabilities |
| Rewards next step | Current entitlement ledger | Explain eligible benefit and redemption | No double earning; returns/reversals remain truthful |
| Message shortcut | Verified merchant relationship and record | Open right thread with context | Private content remains permission-bound |
| Reminder | Committed booking/order and preferences | Actionable deep link | Deduplicate across channels; revoke stale reminders |

## Search that helps rather than guesses

Search can match merchant, offer, category and area. Show useful correction/alternative queries rather than silently changing intent. Preserve entered words and selected filters. A category suggestions row is optional, not another compulsory signup survey. Recent searches are removable and privacy-scoped. Clear-all and reset make their scope obvious: query only versus all filters. Online-only intent does not demand location; “comes to me” checks coverage separately from visitable premises. Mixed physical/digital offers show the delivery mode clearly.

Ranking first excludes ineligible/hidden/paused content, then considers relevance and permitted contextual signals. Paid placement, if introduced, is labelled. Users can inspect/change the area and filters responsible for their result. Do not make unmaintained “best”, “popular” or availability claims. Recommendation quality, cold starts, new-business fairness and bias need actual evaluation; no opaque invented score becomes product truth.

## Cross-device and cross-account continuity

Public browsing state can remain locally. Private drafts, carts, history and conversations belong to the verified principal; guest-to-account transfer needs an explicit secure claim/merge with conflict handling. Shared-device sign-out removes private caches, notifications and draft exposure. Device switching can resume server-backed tasks when supported; local-only drafts are labelled honestly. Merchant carts stay separate. Show which business a bag belongs to; never promise one unified payment across unrelated merchants without the required commercial/payment model.

## Smart defaults are reversible

Remember deliberately selected language, appearance and suitable fulfilment preferences within consent/retention rules. Recommend, explain and allow change. Never infer sensitive needs, auto-enrol memberships, auto-charge, choose a replacement item or release an address because it seems convenient. Keep a calm empty state for a new customer; no invented sample bookings in their real Activity. Personalisation opt-out leaves full core functionality.

Review a first-time browser, direct NFC visitor, returning customer with one merchant, customer with many active transactions, guest completing a booking, expired draft and shared-device account switch. Convenience is demonstrated through fewer repeated decisions and recoverable actions, not through extra dashboards.
