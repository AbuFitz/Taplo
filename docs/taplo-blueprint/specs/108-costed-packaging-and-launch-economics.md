## What the calculation says

The prior prices were anchors, not a cost model. This pass adds researched supplier inputs, explicit assumptions, 12 price/bundle combinations, detailed costs, cohort/free subsidy, acquisition and sensitivity calculations. Results are in ECONOMICS-RESULTS.md, generated by contracts/calculate-economics.py from economics-assumptions.json; raw results are in economics-results.json. Change inputs and rerun rather than manually inventing a margin.

With baseline assumptions and a modest included communications bundle, £12/£24/£49 leaves about £4.60/£7.74/£15.42 per paid account before Free subsidy, shared overhead, acquisition and engineering. At the stated 1,000-paid scenario it still loses about £677 after those specifically modelled items and a £3,000 engineering allowance. It is not proven sustainable just because each subscription exceeds its email bill.

Working recommendation for a more defensible all-package, bundled launch model: **Free £0 / Core £15 / Pro £29 / Business £59 monthly**, headline inclusive of applicable VAT. These are cost-informed proposals, not validated market-clearing prices. Retain £9.99/£19.99/£39.99 and £12/£24/£49 as comparison scenarios. A cheaper ladder requires measured lower support/free subsidy/servicing cost or a different bundle—not confidence alone.

## Customer-facing package design

| Plan | Monthly test price | Useful included communication allowance | Main reason to choose |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Free | £0 | Operational email for accepted work; no included SMS or bulk promotional sends | Genuine branded mini-space, five live offers, 30 new commitments/month, basic booking/orders/enquiry, eligible deposits, QR, private customer return and safe team access |
| Core | £15 | Operational email, 250 marketing email sends, 25 UK SMS units/month | Daily supported operations, catalogue/stock/fulfilment, scheduling/calendar connection, owned-domain connection and richer appearance |
| Pro | £29 | Operational email, 1,000 marketing email sends, 75 UK SMS units/month | Return/growth tools, supported rewards/Wallet, advanced series/packages/quote automation, analytics and controlled templates |
| Business | £59 | Operational email, 3,000 marketing email sends, 150 UK SMS units/month | Up to three managed locations, delegated/custom administration, cross-location views, advanced exports/automation and realistically staffed priority handling |

No per-team-member or ordinary bookable-member fee in any tier. Basic permissions/revocation, eligible deposit protection, lawful refunds/data access and management of accepted work are not optional paid safeguards. Package scope must be fully supported before it is advertised/sold: all packages planned at launch does not authorise selling fictional functionality.

The low SMS allowances are deliberate. A UK SMS unit is one provider-billed segment, not an appointment or arbitrarily long message. Usually one compact reminder can fit one segment, but encoding/length changes the count. Show the actual cost before sending; don't require the owner to understand GSM encoding. This is a customer-value tradeoff to test, not a claim it beats competitors' messaging bundles for every operator.

Core/Pro/Business retain their feature allocation from 104 unless superseded here; core safe operations remain useful without needing all growth tools. Free allowance/metering guards in 104 still apply. All packages launch monthly; no annual discount or free hardware/domain-registration promise until costs/terms support it.

## Base operating assumptions

Full included SMS and marketing-email allowance is used every month. Operational emails: 150/500/1,500 per paid account. Variable infrastructure: £0.80/£1.50/£3.00; human support: 5/10/20 minutes/month at £0.50/minute; SaaS loss/admin reserve: £0.10/£0.20/£0.40. These are rough planning assumptions, not measured loads, wage statistics or actuarial loss rates. Actual cloud use, large galleries, maps, realtime/queues, search, logs/backup and support can exceed them.

Active Free account assumed cost £0.45/month: £0.15 variable infrastructure, £0.03 email, half a support minute (£0.25), £0.02 other. Model four active Free accounts per paying account. Dormant accounts may be cheaper; active heavy accounts may be far dearer. No support time is treated as free just because the founder answers.

Paid mix 60% Core/30% Pro/10% Business. Shared operating budget £150/£300/£500 at 100/500/1,000 paid is an assumed platform/tool/admin pool, not a full quoted company budget. It excludes separately stated variable serving costs to avoid double counting; real supplier bill allocation must be reconciled. Engineering, legal/accounting/insurance/acquisition/general costs beyond that pool are not secretly included.

## Growth and break-even reality

At the selected mix and four active Free per paid: 100 paid produces £2,360 gross subscription billing, 500 produces £11,800, 1,000 produces £23,600. After VAT, direct paid serving, Free subsidy and shared budgets, about £681/£3,856/£7,812 remains before acquisition/engineering.

Assume £30 to acquire a paid merchant, 20 minutes onboarding at £0.50/min and 5% monthly paid churn. Maintaining the cohort costs another £200/£1,000/£2,000/month; this does not fund extra growth acquisition. Left before engineering is then about £481/£2,856/£5,812. After a further £3,000 engineering allowance: about -£2,519/-£144/£2,812. Founder salary and other excluded company costs still matter; this is not distributable profit or a growth forecast.

Estimated acquisition-plus-onboarding payback is about 4.8 months after allocated Free cost, before shared/engineering costs, under constant base assumptions. Churn, conversion, acquisition cost and paid mix are unvalidated inputs. See full scale/free/support/SMS/cloud/email sensitivities in ECONOMICS-RESULTS rather than quoting only the favourable row.

## Why not enormous message bundles?

500 SMS at the 5p assumption costs £25 before everything else. Increasing Core/Pro/Business to 50/150/300 units at the lower £9.99/£19.99/£39.99 ladder leaves extremely little contribution under this model. Making it look all-inclusive does not remove cost. Actual providers with lower confirmed rates or lower observed utilisation can change the result; do not assume negotiation savings before they exist.

Booksy's current UK bundle includes a substantial messaging/features offer [R73]. For a solo operator sending many reminders, it can be more attractive than Taplo plus top-ups even if Taplo's base price is lower. For a team, included staff and the identity/commerce/return proposition may matter more. Compare actual total monthly usage in the package selector; no blanket cheapest claim.

## Operating gates and refinement

Measure real support time, cloud/asset/search volume, channel delivery/segment mix, failures, refunds, active Free cost, conversion/retention and acquisition before locking public prices. Full allowance use is conservative for messaging, but infrastructure/support assumptions may be optimistic. If the audience will not pay the cost-informed prices, reduce avoidable cost/scope/friction or revise the model; do not promise a perfect margin or assume prices can simply be raised later without retention impact.

Founder instruction is explicit: all four packages planned for launch. Their supported feature set, entitlement/metering, billing, upgrade/downgrade, message allowances, support and failure handling must be complete together. The contract below makes extras understandable; it does not replace meaningful beta/pilot evidence before general sale.
