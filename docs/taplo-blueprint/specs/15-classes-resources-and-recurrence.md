# Sessions, resources, recurrence and mobile work

Status: specialist capability design. Depends on: 13–14.

A group class is a scheduled Session with an instructor, place/resources, capacity and attendee reservations. It is not many identical staff appointments layered on one time. Track seat quantity, attendee identity where needed, payer, attendance and cancellation independently.

Waitlist: record preference → notify an eligible customer or group → time-limited invitation → reserve seats → confirm → expire and offer onwards. Define fairness and avoid two people believing they own one released seat. No automatic paid conversion without an agreed purchase mechanism.

Recurrence: store a series rule and exceptions. Actions distinguish this occurrence, future occurrences and the series. Display the total price/commitment and what a cancellation changes. An instructor or room conflict on one date must not be hidden by the recurring rule.

Resources include rooms, stations or equipment with separate capacity and eligibility. Processing time can release staff while retaining a chair/room where the business model requires it. Mobile services add service areas, travel buffers and address-dependent feasibility. These are capabilities, not changes to page labels.

Acceptance: a customer buys two seats when only one remains and receives a clear correction. Cancelling an instructor identifies all affected sessions. A refund for one attendee does not delete the session. A recurring booking spanning DST retains intended local time. A resource held during processing time cannot be double-booked even when the staff member is free.
