The page is composed of typed widgets rather than a conventional website builder's unrestricted sections. Every widget has a stable instance ID, type, operational source references where needed, presentation settings, visibility/order and optional style overrides. Its operational record is separate: duplicating a product widget does not duplicate stock, price or customer agreements.

## Proposed block families

| Family | Compact public view | Expanded interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Logo/name, one short introduction, relevant area/delivery and chosen contact actions | Profile/contact sheet when needed |
| Primary action | Clear task with one useful supporting line | Correct quote, booking, enquiry or contact action |
| Services | Short price list, tiles or a small shelf; owner chooses relevant subset | Full service detail and eligible booking/request flow |
| Products/stock | Horizontal cards with image, concise name, price and one useful fact | Options, detail, delivery/fitting context, native cart and related products |
| Photos/work | Compact filmstrip or bounded mosaic | Image/project viewer with meaningful captions |
| Team | Short identity cards or shelf | Person, role and eligible services |
| Reviews/proof | A concise genuine quote/summary or review-link block | More permitted reviews or external source with clear destination |
| Hours/location/area | One useful fact and current context | Full hours, directions or actual service-area information |
| FAQ/about/download/video | Short entry with useful summary | Read/view/download in the right contextual surface |
| Rewards | Clear merchant-specific balance/progress when authorised | Terms, available rewards and redemption path |
| Links/social | Compact actions with recognisable destination | The chosen external destination; do not forbid legitimate external links |

## Shelf behaviour

Product shelves have bounded height, deliberate card width and a visible next-card peek. Use native horizontal scrolling and snap with no autoplay. Display previous/next controls when useful, meaningful item names and an optional count; do not make touch gestures the only route. Vertical page scrolling must continue normally when a gesture is predominantly vertical.

A card shows enough to choose: image, understandable name, price basis and concise distinguishing fact. Do not display a full paragraph, repeated labelled facts and several competing CTAs below every product on the landing page. The product popup contains that detail. For large catalogues, “View all” opens a searchable/filterable collection sheet or equivalent focused collection view; the public widget stays compact.

Services need not all be horizontal. A short list can be more scannable for named prices; a tile or shelf can suit image-led services. Set a chosen compact subset with an explicit “All services” action. Avoid hiding important service distinctions behind repeated vague icons.

## States and acceptance

Zero items: suppress the empty public widget and give the owner an actionable source-content prompt. One item: remove meaningless carousel controls and use a suitable one-item treatment. Many items: preserve card size and page height. Missing image: intentional text/icon composition, not an empty grey box. Sold out: visible truthful state, no add action that implies purchase eligibility.

Acceptance: adding twenty products does not add twenty vertical public cards. Opening and closing a product restores the same shelf position. Cards remain keyboard reachable at 320px and zoom. Product visibility, price and stock agree with the source record and detail. A duplicated showcase still uses the same offer identity.
