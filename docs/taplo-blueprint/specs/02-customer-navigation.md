# Customer navigation and existing relationships

Current continuation · 2.4: CUSTOMER-WEB-AND-ACQUISITION, COMMUNICATIONS-AND-MARKETING, TRUST-REVIEWS-AND-REPORTING and LEGAL-PACK-INDEX deepen these rules. Their supplier, consent, review/report and publication decisions supersede earlier open-ended wording. Read the explicit evidence and legal readiness limits.

Status: proposed journey. Depends on: 01, 03, 18.

Recommend four customer destinations: Explore, Saved, Activity and Account. Keep messages attached to their relevant activity initially; add a dedicated Inbox only when message volume justifies another destination.

Explore is public browsing and search. Saved holds deliberately saved businesses and offers. Activity holds bookings, orders, quotes, subscriptions and open issues. Account holds verified identity, addresses, privacy and notification preferences. Buying from a business does not silently mean consenting to marketing or following it.

A returning customer should see the next useful action: “Your class is tomorrow”, “Your collection is ready” or “Your quote needs a decision”. Rank by actual urgency and date, not invented engagement. After that action, link back to the business mini-site.

Direct mini-site visitors do not receive a compulsory global app shell. From Explore/Saved, show a small contextual back bar above the business experience. During booking or checkout, replace browsing navigation with task-specific Back/Cancel controls. Restore origin, filters and scroll on return.

Acceptance: a customer saves a business, visits from Saved and sees the same identity and offers as from its NFC link. A past purchase stays in Activity after unfollowing. A customer can browse without location permission and enter a town manually. A logged-out private link requests verification and returns to its exact task afterwards. Signing out removes private cached content.

Open: whether a returning-customer Home becomes justified. Avoid adding Home, Discover, Explore and Feed as separate destinations with overlapping purposes.
