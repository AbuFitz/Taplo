# 88 · The private return view inside a business space

Working edition 1.3 · 3 November 2026 · Proposed experience to prototype. Extends 02, 18, 56, 64 and 81. No new observed user validation is claimed.

## Public home and private continuation

Keep one merchant mini-space. When an authorised returning customer has relevant activity, show a compact For you section near the top, below one principal merchant identity and before appropriate public blocks. It does not replace the page with a generic portal or repeat the logo/name across every card. Public products/services stay in their compact horizontal rails.

First show the most useful next task: upcoming appointment, order ready, accepted quote awaiting deposit, or genuine payment action required. A small View your activity action opens the merchant-filtered private detail. Rewards are secondary unless the visitor intentionally opens them; do not turn a useful appointment view into a promotional dashboard.

## Access states

| Visitor | Visible content | Access path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anonymous public visitor | Published business blocks; neutral optional access link | No disclosure that a named person has an appointment |
| Guest with scoped management link | Only the authorised task and necessary details | Verify link/access as required; no automatic access to all matching email records |
| Signed-in verified customer | Permitted relationship records for this business | Account ownership plus record/participant authority, not name matching |
| Guardian/payer/learner | Only records/fields each relationship permits | Separate permission model; payer not automatically entitled to confidential learner notes |
| Shared device or expired session | Public space and access recovery | Clear private cache; no personal facts left in public preview |

The same appointment can be reached from Activity, reminder, merchant page or website action. These are navigation contexts over one source record. Cross-business Activity remains available in the Taplo customer app; the business view stays scoped to that business. A merchant must never learn which unrelated businesses a customer uses.

## Card content and task details

Appointment card: service, date/time with suitable timezone, professional/location where relevant, verified status and one useful action. Order card: reference, fulfilment status and collection/tracking action. Balance card: exact outstanding value and why due; hide when settled or uncertain rather than fabricating an amount. Quote card: current version/expiry and Review; rewards card: real balance/eligibility, not decorative earned points.

Tap opens an elevated rounded detail surface with neutral contrast, scoped merchant accent and clear heading/close affordance. Mobile full-height task view is available when content requires it; desktop can use a restrained modal/detail region. Do not stack several bottom sheets for payment → policy → help → confirmation. Use one task context with internal steps, predictable Back and restored scroll/focus.

Private detail includes current status, next action, authorised change/cancel, financial summary, applicable instructions, linked messages and support route. Internal merchant notes, staff-only fields, other customers' names and sensitive intake never appear just because they share the booking record. Timed links recheck authority, not merely possession of a predictable booking ID.

## Ranking and exceptions

Rank actionable issues first, then nearest relevant upcoming activity, then useful repeat/rewards. Do not show a scary overdue banner while payment is processing. Cancellations/refunds remain findable without occupying the main card indefinitely; history is separate. Multiple family bookings show enough participant context without unnecessary sensitive details. Stale offline data is labelled and irreversible/financial actions require fresh validation.

No tasks: retain normal business space and a calm access/manage link; do not create empty cards. Merchant leaves Taplo: preserve appropriate historic/private support access under closure policy, without implying new bookings remain available. Customer signs out: private cards and cached sensitive responses are cleared; public merchant remains browsable.

## Metrics and prototype questions

Measure ability to find/manage the next task, mistaken cancellations, payment confusion, support contacts and useful repeat actions. Compare a compact For you card against a separate Your activity entry; neither should obscure merchant customisation. Test guest links, shared device, family roles, several appointments, pending refund and no activity. This feature's value is a hypothesis; approve the composition after actual task testing, not a feature-count argument.
