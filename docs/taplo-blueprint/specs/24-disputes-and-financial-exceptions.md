# Complaints, returns and card disputes are separate workflows

Status: proposed support contract; processor configuration and legal review required.

Classify four common tracks: merchant product/service complaint or return; card-network payment dispute; Taplo subscription/billing complaint; Taplo hardware-order issue. A general Report a problem entry can route them without forcing customers to understand the internal distinction.

Merchant complaint: capture issue and requested outcome → notify responsible merchant → record response → proposed resolution → customer update → close/escalate. A return follows fulfilment and applicable rights. Taplo can facilitate within its role; it must not promise an automatic favourable financial outcome.

A card dispute is governed by the processor/network process. Ingest the authoritative dispute, deadline and amount; identify responsible merchant/platform role; collect reason-specific evidence; accept or contest through authorised actions; submit before the actual deadline; show provider status and outcome. The issuer decides the outcome. Do not reuse a single fixed deadline or fee across countries and networks.

Evidence may include agreed scope, policy acceptance, receipt, fulfilment/attendance proof, customer communications and prior refunds. Minimise sensitive material and restrict retention/access. Prevent conflicting refund and dispute actions using the current processor rules; do not double-compensate through disconnected tools.

Acceptance: a complaint can remain unresolved while a refund is pending, and the UI says so. A provider deadline appears in Console with clear ownership. A partial dispute links to relevant lines and prior refunds. Customer-facing “Resolved” identifies what changed; it does not imply a bank decision that has not happened.
