# Explore: useful discovery without replacing the business home

Status: product hypothesis. Evidence: current Shop and Spaces customer patterns in source record.

Explore uses compact, branded business windows containing a few relevant offer actions; it does not reproduce every mini-site widget. Spec 44 defines the current interaction direction. Start with meaningful facts: business identity, category, area or delivery reach, relevant offer and current availability where trustworthy. For products, shipping/collection matters; for mobile services, service area matters; for online lessons, time zone matters. “Near me” cannot be the universal discovery model.

Search should understand intent through explicit filters before relying on AI: what, where/online, when, price suitability and offer type. Show why a result matches. Label sponsored placement if introduced. Keep private customer information out of search indexing.

Published does not automatically mean Explore-listed. Owners can choose public discoverability subject to eligibility, content quality and platform rules. Suspended or withdrawn offers disappear from relevant discovery views promptly. Opening a result leads into the canonical branded mini-site or deep-linked offer.

Early discovery needs useful supply. Begin with a bounded category/location or curated use case; let direct merchant links produce value even when Explore has little coverage. Do not present fictional availability or a misleading busy marketplace.

Acceptance: search for an online tutor without location permission; search for a workshop with seats on a chosen date; return from a result without losing filters. Empty results explain alternatives without silently changing the requested area. A business's own page contains no automatic competing-business insertion. Discovery measurement distinguishes impressions, meaningful visits and completed customer actions.
