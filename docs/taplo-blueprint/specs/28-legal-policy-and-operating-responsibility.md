# Policy work that belongs in the product design

Current continuation · 2.4: CUSTOMER-WEB-AND-ACQUISITION, COMMUNICATIONS-AND-MARKETING, TRUST-REVIEWS-AND-REPORTING and LEGAL-PACK-INDEX deepen these rules. Their supplier, consent, review/report and publication decisions supersede earlier open-ended wording. Read the explicit evidence and legal readiness limits.

Status: requirements map, not final legal advice or ready-to-publish terms.

Decide initial jurisdictions, contracting entities, supported merchant types and payment arrangement. Define who sells each offer, who supplies it, who handles returns/refunds, who bears relevant payment liabilities and what Taplo does when a merchant stops responding. Terms, checkout labels, receipts, Console permissions and support processes must agree.

For UK online selling, use current official distance-selling guidance when designing required pre-contract information, total costs, confirmations and cancellation handling. Different goods/services and exceptions require distinct treatment. A generic checkbox or “no refunds” template is not an adequate policy engine.

Separate business subscription terms, merchant-to-customer policies, Taplo hardware terms, acceptable-use rules, privacy/cookie notices, rewards terms and complaints/escalation policies. Provide editable merchant facts with safe validation; do not generate invented business addresses or credentials.

Subscription design must cover trial, paid renewal, upgrade/downgrade, usage charges if any, failed payment, cancellation, data export and wind-down access. Do not punish customers with lost receipts because the merchant's subscription failed.

Acceptance: the legal seller is clear before purchase. The accepted policy version is retrievable with the transaction. A merchant policy change affects future commitments appropriately and flags any proposed change to existing ones. Owners see their actual obligations in plain language at the relevant step. Final public policies receive suitable professional review before release.
