# Quoted work and repeat-value offers

Status: proposed capabilities, not all required for first release.

Quoted work: customer submits a scoped brief with useful attachments and availability → business clarifies → quote version is issued → customer accepts exact scope/price/terms → required deposit → scheduling/work → balance/invoice → completion. A quote change creates a new version rather than modifying an accepted agreement in place.

An enquiry is a question, not an order. A booking request is a request for time, not a quote acceptance. Shared conversations can connect them, but labels and commitments must remain clear.

Packages hold entitlements such as five eligible sessions; memberships hold recurring billing and access rules. Define start/end, expiry, eligible offers, transferability, cancellation, payment failure, pause and refunds. A reserved session consumes or reserves an entitlement according to explicit rules, and reversal restores the correct entitlement once.

Proposed useful feature: “Continue where you left off” on the private activity, taking the customer straight to an unanswered quote question or remaining balance. Avoid a generic pay link detached from the agreement.

Acceptance: an expired quote cannot be accepted at an old price without owner approval. A parent can pay for an identified learner without that learner seeing the payer's financial details. Concurrent bookings cannot spend the same final package credit twice. Cancelling a membership does not erase its receipts or falsely cancel already-paid future commitments without an explicit rule.
