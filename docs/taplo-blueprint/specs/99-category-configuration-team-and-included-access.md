# 99 · Category configuration, team operations and included access

Working edition 1.8 · 3 November 2026 · Adopted working rules extending 62, 64, 71–73 and 91. These deepen category differences; they do not certify all specialist workflows complete.

## Pricing direction: team access included

Do not charge merely for an extra staff login or bookable team member within a merchant workspace. This is the adopted working packaging direction, not a final subscription price or a claim of unlimited infrastructure/support. Team permissions, individual attribution, revocation and essential financial safeguards are core behaviour, not an upgrade needed to operate safely.

The strategic rationale is simplicity: owners can invite the people who actually do the work rather than share passwords or calculate a growing seat bill. This is a business hypothesis, not established conversion/retention evidence. More people can still create real support, messages, storage, integration and location complexity; cost those usage drivers explicitly. Do not disguise a staff fee as a new calendar/resource fee while advertising no team charges. Separate independent merchant workspaces or a genuinely additional service are different commercial objects and must be explained plainly.

Do not promise unlimited organisations/locations/SMS/storage/managed onboarding. Any package boundaries must reflect actual scope/cost and be declared before purchase; do not invent numeric caps now. Security/abuse controls apply without penalising ordinary legitimate teams. Re-evaluate economics with solo, small-team, busy multi-location and support-heavy scenarios before publishing prices.

## Shared permission spine, category-specific work

Identity, Membership, Resource and TeamProfile are separate. A business can schedule a resource without granting it login access; a login can perform fulfilment without a public profile. Grants combine action + scope + field rules. Category selects a sensible preset and terminology, not a new independent authorisation engine. Existing 31 granular actions remain the baseline; missing specialist actions require explicit additions, never a manager boolean bypass.

Owner configures: what work this person performs, eligible offers, locations/resources, calendar/customer visibility, own-profile editing, permitted operational actions, money privileges and any approval requirements. Show a plain-language access summary and navigation preview before an invitation. Role title does not itself authorise everything with the same name in another business.

## Five deeper team workflows

**Barber:** owner creates person/resource, assigns services/location, chooses login and profile separately, reviews access, sends invitation deliberately. Staff accepts into My work and optionally My profile; edits permitted bio/gallery as draft. Owner publishes profile, sets price/policy and manages team rules. Staff absence first checks accepted work; reassignment checks eligibility and named-professional agreement. Departure revokes sessions/grants, resolves future appointments, archives/reassigns profile link deliberately and keeps agreement history.

**Tutoring:** coordinator links tutor to assigned learners/sessions and limited reports. Parent/payer and learner are distinct participants, not tags on the same account. Tutor can submit permitted attendance/report without exporting the whole client list or seeing another tutor's confidential notes. Removing a tutor resolves recurring sessions, joining links and retained reports without deleting historic attendance. Any specialist safeguarding workflow still requires its own reviewed design.

**Mobile work:** dispatcher assigns technician and planned arrival scope. Technician sees necessary private location/intake, can record progress and upload relevant evidence. Scope variation generates a customer decision; it cannot mutate an accepted price. Calendar availability includes travel; dispatcher cannot create an impossible overlap simply by selecting a different status. Completion/collection permissions and financial authority remain separate. Reassignment revokes unnecessary old access while retaining attributed evidence.

**Retail:** fulfilment staff sees eligible assigned/location orders and necessary packing/collection facts, not marketing audiences or payout accounts. Preparation, part collection and dispatch update actual line quantities. A return needs a disposition decision before increasing sellable stock. Stock adjustment and refund are separate grants/actions; manager title alone grants neither without the configured policy. Staff departure removes device/session and export access.

**Workshop:** instructor sees assigned session roster, attendance and necessary support information; organiser handles date/capacity changes, host substitution and notices. Reduced capacity cannot delete already sold places. Waiting-list contact is a scoped operational process, not a marketing list. Cancelling a session reviews all affected attendees/payment obligations, not merely hides the public block.

## Common access lifecycle and edge cases

Pending invite, resend/expiry, existing Taplo user, multi-business user, limited role, temporary location assignment, permission change mid-session, leave, suspension, lost device and departure all need explicit states. Switching business clears unrelated cached private content and resolves grants anew. Owner access recovery/ownership transfer are distinct protected actions. Financial exports, bank changes and broad customer export cannot be granted by a colourful role badge.

Preset mapping in contracts/category-team-presets.json is a proposed selection of existing templates plus obligations; it is not runtime ACL enforcement. Before construction, bind specialist field masks/actions and validate through actual API/realtime/export/job paths. Team access included changes pricing, not privacy boundaries.
