## Separate search purposes

Taplo marketing pages answer why a business should adopt the platform. Merchant public pages answer what that actual business offers and how a customer can act. Explore pages help customers find genuine eligible businesses. Studio, Console and private customer tasks are protected applications, not search landing pages. Their content/metadata must never be reused as public SEO material.

| Surface | Search purpose | Initial page/intent examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Taplo homepage | Explain the proposition and route a relevant visitor | Compact business page, useful actions, easy customer return |
| Capability pages | Answer concrete workflow needs with actual proof | Online booking with deposits; simple product sales; team calendars; QR/NFC business sharing |
| Main-site solution showcases | Explain meaningful category fit, not swap a noun in one template | Barber team/booking workflow; mobile-service intake/quotes; workshop capacity |

| Comparison pages | Help a specific purchasing decision | Current like-for-like tasks, geography/currency/date and source-backed limits |
| Merchant pages/items | Present actual published business/offer facts | Business identity, service/product/class details and eligible actions |

These are seed intents/editorial directions, not researched search-volume figures or proven demand. Before commissioning a large content set, map actual queries/results, intent, competition, distinct value and achievable content quality. Do not manufacture traffic forecasts.

## Marketing narrative and copy defaults

Working lead: **Your business, easy to choose. Easy to come back to.** Supporting copy: **Create a page that feels like you. Let customers book, buy or get in touch—and keep the next step easy.** Alternative short line for evaluation: **A better link to your business.** These are original working copy options, not cleared trademarks or tested conversion winners.

Show, in order: a real compact business space → one customer action → simple owner work → returning customer utility → proof relevant to the category → clear package/start action. Use genuine screenshots or accurate labelled synthetic examples, not floating abstract mockups that imply impossible functionality. Avoid platform for everyone, unlock your potential, revolutionary all-in-one or claims of guaranteed growth.

Explain the existing-website use case, native commerce, optional discovery and included team access plainly. Do not lead every page with the internal product architecture or list a hundred planned capabilities. The same public wording should reflect the intended finished product, while release publication only claims genuinely available material capabilities. Internal readiness belongs in the blueprint, not permanent customer copy.

## Technical publication contract

Return correct public HTML with meaningful title, heading, main content and crawlable links. Don't depend on a visitor opening a JavaScript-only popup for the only readable offer information; public item routes can render useful equivalent detail while in-app navigation opens the task surface. Use actual status codes for missing/deleted/unavailable content, stable IDs/redirects for moved handles, canonical rules per chosen public host and consistent internal links. Google's JavaScript guidance explains crawler/rendering constraints. [R82]

Public metadata, visible facts, structured data, sitemaps and share previews derive from the same published record revision. Draft/example/private records are excluded. No infinite query/filter/viewport URL space from Explore or the Studio preview. Curate indexable category/location collections only where genuine supply and useful distinct content exist; other filter states have a deliberate crawl/index policy. Robots controls, canonical and noindex do different jobs. [R86–R87]

Merchant custom domain and Taplo-hosted equivalent need one explicit primary/canonical/redirect strategy per route. Don't duplicate full content on several hosts then hope metadata settles it automatically. Primary existing website can remain authoritative for broad brand information while Taplo has distinct useful task pages; never canonicalise every service to an unrelated homepage.

Private pages require real access control. noindex is not security; robots.txt alone neither protects data nor reliably removes a known URL. Metadata and error pages for private tasks must be generic and public-safe. [R87] Published assets/documents also need the correct access policy, not only the HTML route.

## Structured data and automated owner assistance

Generate supported structured data from real visible facts and eligible types, not invented descriptions/ratings/hours. A home-based/mobile provider's private address must not become public JSON-LD merely to satisfy a LocalBusiness field. If required public facts/eligibility are absent, omit that enhancement rather than fabricate it. Structured data creates eligibility, not guaranteed search features. [R83]

Owner guidance can suggest clear titles/descriptions from authored category/location/offer data, show a search-preview approximation and flag missing public facts. Owner can review phrasing. Do not infer credentials, all-day opening, cheapest price, reviews or city coverage. Do not sell keyword stuffing or produce pages for every UK postcode from a radius.

## Content quality and governance

Research should produce useful first-hand product demonstrations, owner/task insights and accurate explanations. Content has an accountable author/reviewer, source/date for factual comparisons, version and update/retirement trigger. Legal/payment guidance links qualified/current sources and states scope; no generated universal legal policy. Claims about competitors use current evidence and precise region/plan, not old stereotypes.

Google recommends people-first useful content and prohibits scaled low-value content intended to manipulate rankings. [R81, R85] Don't spin one article into hundreds of near-identical category/city pages. A reusable page system is good engineering; each published page still needs distinctive useful content. Accessibility, performance, readable images and stable mobile layouts support the actual visitor; a technical score alone is not an organic acquisition strategy.

## Tools and monitoring

Use Search Console for Taplo-owned verified properties, sitemap/index/coverage and query evidence; a merchant custom domain requires appropriate ownership/permission. Use structured-data validators, PageSpeed/Lighthouse-style performance checks and a bounded crawl to inspect status/title/canonical/internal links. Inspect actual mobile rendering and real tasks alongside automated reports. A paid keyword platform is optional after defining the question; tool access is not research completed.

Track non-brand versus brand discovery, relevant landing-page visits, qualified draft starts, completed useful setup, retained merchants and support costs by cohort where lawful. Traffic without suitable buyers is not success. Review which queries/pages help users, where pages are indexed but ineffective, and where examples become stale. Do not claim click-through or search-position improvement until measured.
