# 85 · Market patterns, opportunities and measurement

Working edition 1.2 · 3 November 2026 · Focused research expansion, not an exhaustive competitor audit or validated market demand. Current primary-source findings and Taplo hypotheses are separate.

## What the research changes

Do not position established platforms using old descriptions such as booking only or links only. Study an actual connected task, current controls, failure cases and customer return experience. A comparison homepage is not proof of an absent competitor capability or a dissatisfied customer population. No claim of unique invention, cheaper operation or better conversion is established here.

## Taplo's proposed edge

One merchant-authored offer can appear in a custom mini-space, website action link, staff profile, customer return card and physical touchpoint, while retaining one operational record. Studio adapts the configuration to the work rather than making every merchant administer an ecommerce store. Familiar public blocks and concise task popups lead into dependable booking/order/quote operations and private continuation.

This is a coherent proposition to test, not a guaranteed moat. Its value depends on whether merchants can set it up, customers complete tasks, support can recover errors, and usage costs remain viable.

## Analytics that inform decisions

Separate acquisition source, task conversion, operational outcome and financial outcome. Merchant analytics must not claim a website referral equals a new customer, a paid deposit equals total realised revenue, or a message delivered equals read. Staff-link attribution must not become unrestricted access to customer histories or an employment-performance score.

Proposed event dictionary requires event ID, schema version, tenant, permitted actor/session scope, occurred/received timestamps, purpose and non-sensitive source. Private tokens, addresses, calendar titles, health/intake answers and message bodies stay out. Cross-device attribution and marketing tracking require their applicable privacy/consent design; do not circumvent rejection by relabelling analytics essential.

Core measures: draft-to-first-useful-offer, setup errors/assistance, publish readiness, offer-to-eligible-selection, checkout success/failure, time/payment mismatches, no-show and cancellation outcomes, refund completion time, repeat task completion, reminder delivery failures and support contacts per completed task. Define denominator/cohort/window for each before building charts. Refunds, unpaid balances and fees need separate financial views, not a single inflated revenue number.

## Research backlog with a question, not a logo list

Expand primary-source study into creator commerce (Stan/Beacons/Linktree), appointment and team workflows (Fresha/Booksy/Square), service projects (HoneyBook/Dubsado), classes (Bookwhen/SimplyBook.me), scheduling (SavvyCal/Cal.com), customer loyalty/Wallet, and smaller category-specific tools. For each record exact current workflow, unique useful mechanism, limitation, permissions, costs/dependencies, Taplo adaptation and evidence date. This list is planned research, not completed findings about those products.

Include discontinued/poorly adopted products only when there is reliable evidence of the actual mechanism and outcome. Do not invent failure reasons from appearance or assume a small company proves a weak idea. Public research can improve preparation; observation still tests assumptions that documents cannot establish.

## Experiment gates

Compare website action link versus mini-space entry; customer business-specific return view versus general Activity; category-guided offer setup versus generic form; no-upfront versus clearly disclosed deposit where lawful/appropriate. Set success and harm measures before testing. Do not optimise conversion by hiding cancellation, forcing install or making consent confusing.
