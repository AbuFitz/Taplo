# Guided setup that produces a usable business

Status: recommendation. Depends on: 06, 10.

Begin with the business name, what it offers and how customers receive it. Ask for a category to supply helpful terminology and defaults, then ask only operational questions needed for chosen capabilities. Allow “products and appointments”, “products and workshops” and other combinations.

After a short identity/intent/design draft, open Studio directly into guided first-offer setup (spec 79). The first real offer is part of that connected journey rather than a mandatory extended registration form. A physical item needs price, stock behaviour and fulfilment. An appointment needs duration, delivery/location and availability. Quoted work needs a useful brief and response expectation. Collect required seller/payment information when the chosen transaction requires it, using a reputable processor's onboarding rather than inventing a compliance form.

Show a tailored readiness list: business identity, first offer, how customers receive it, required terms and payment capability where relevant. Let the owner preview the exact customer journey. Publication and paid-transaction eligibility are separate checks; clearly explain any unavailable action in owner language.

Save progress, allow skipping optional enrichment and return to the unfinished step. Do not ask for all staff, products, social links, colours and marketing preferences before the first useful result. Starter content is clearly separate from real business content and cannot leak into unrelated forms.

Acceptance: a solo maker reaches a correct physical-product preview without appointment questions. Adding workshops later asks for scheduling facts without restarting. A trades owner receives a quote workflow rather than an irrelevant stock editor. Every template-supplied enquiry option is derived from that business's actual offers.


Current direction · 3 November 2026: spec 79 governs onboarding/Studio staging. Prices and commercial/operational settings are deliberately entered by the owner, never filled from a website. Content extraction is deferred and, if later justified, a basic-content-only Studio tool.
