# A recognisable Taplo design language

Status: creative direction to prototype and validate; not a trademark or uniqueness claim.

## Recognition comes from the system

Businesses can look different while customers recognise Taplo's construction: a compact identity composition, purposeful widgets, deliberate horizontal shelves, consistent action placement, and a matching popup that opens from the chosen item. The powered-by footer supports recognition but cannot carry it alone.

Recommend developing a small set of reusable signatures:

- Identity compositions with purposeful transitions into the first blocks, including good outcomes for logo + cover, logo only, photo only and neither.
- Consistent relationships between outer widget corners, inner item corners, padding and action areas. Each visual family has coherent values rather than arbitrary per-element rounding.
- A restrained, recognisable action corner on interactive blocks. It signals open/detail/action consistently without giving every non-interactive tile an arrow.
- Shelves whose photography, labels, prices and edge peeks form a compact, rhythmic surface. Different content families get intentional proportions.
- A shared sheet header and action-area structure with an independent neutral task surface with business accent and compatible type. The product feels continuous as the customer moves from block to action.
- Precise, brief response to tapping, selecting, adding and returning. Reduced-motion versions preserve meaning. Avoid decorative page entrances that slow access.

These are proposals for actual design work, not proof that a rounded card alone is distinctive. Do not force one geometric gimmick on every content type or require every merchant to use Taplo's own brand colour.

## Editable and dependable layers

Owners can change compositions, colours, imagery, typography families, coordinated surfaces, block variants, ordering and supported local emphasis. Advanced adjustments remain possible with clear scope/reset. Taplo governs accessible behaviour, price/selection clarity, safe transaction hierarchy, source consistency, keyboard/Back/Close semantics and minimum interaction affordances.

Category influences useful content and operating rules. It should not force beauty businesses into a prescribed pink page or trades into a prescribed yellow page. A founder, tutor or shop owner should be able to choose restrained or expressive looks without losing the platform's interaction identity.

## Recognition test

Build two genuinely different merchant looks with the same block/action grammar. Hide the footer and logo in a test and ask people to identify shared patterns after using both. Also test whether they can find services, inspect a product, return, change an option and find the cart without coaching. Recognition that costs usability or merchant identity is not success.

Acceptance: the same product popup hierarchy works in dark, light and more expressive coordinated looks. A sharp/minimal style does not become randomly pill-shaped in checkout. The customer can predict which widgets open and what type of action follows. The mini-site remains compact with 3, 12 and 40 products because the shelf owns catalogue breadth.
