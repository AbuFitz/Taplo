# 76 · Policy evaluation, acceptance and legal change

Edition 1.0 · Proposed engineering/workflow contract to support reviewed policies. It does not generate final legal clauses or decide statutory applicability automatically.

## Policy layers

Applicable legal requirements → platform agreement/standards → merchant offer/transaction policy → specific accepted agreement. Lower layers cannot waive non-excludable rights or override actual seller responsibility. Separate software subscription, merchant supply, hardware, domain, reward/member terms, acceptable use, privacy and marketing choices. Do not bundle optional marketing consent into required contract acceptance.

PolicyVersion stores responsible entity, purpose/type, jurisdiction/supported scope, version, effective time, reviewed status, content reference/hash and superseded version. PolicyAcceptance records relevant principal/authority, agreement/record, version and content hash, presented action/copy version, time and evidence reference. Acceptance evidence is minimised and access-controlled; it is not a licence to collect unnecessary fingerprinting data.

## Commitment evaluation

Before buy/book/subscribe: resolve actual seller and offer mode → evaluate supported jurisdiction/category → calculate full price/fees/tax/fulfilment → display material terms and applicable required choices → allow correction → capture specific agreement/acceptance → commit through command/payment rules → issue durable confirmation. Preview must show the same terms/version that commit uses. If they change materially before submit, show the difference and request a fresh deliberate review.

Before cancellation/reschedule/no-show fee: resolve actor/contract/time → evaluate applicable rights and accepted reviewed rule → calculate operational/financial impact without double recovery → display exact consequence → confirm authorised action → apply guarded transition and financial/credit operation → durable record/communication. A merchant cannot use a styling control to disguise a paid commitment as a free enquiry. Staff can request an exception under permission, not edit historical terms.

## Provider and professional review boundaries

Save proposed policies as drafts. Enable an offer only when required merchant facts, supported configuration and relevant review/acceptance requirements are present. Do not run a generic “AI legal generator” that publishes unsupported clauses for regulated/specialist work. Track reviewer/owner and scope; a policy reviewed for one country/service is not automatically approved for every business category. Include a mechanism to flag potential unfair cancellation settings rather than claim an automated validator can conclusively determine fairness.

## Legal change workflow

Identify source/effective date → determine affected activities/territories → professional interpretation → version policy/configuration → preview affected future commitments and existing agreement treatment → implement screens/commands/notices/support → communicate where required → test → release on the correct effective schedule → retain old agreement versions. Current subscription commencement ambiguity is recorded in spec 74; release work must resolve it rather than choose a convenient date.

## Acceptance scenarios

Old booking retains its accepted policy after merchant changes future terms. Marketing withdrawal stops eligible campaigns without cancelling a paid booking. No-show action cannot charge without the applicable authority/reviewed rule. Consumer cancellation path can differ from merchant cancellation and SaaS cancellation. Refund processing pending is communicated accurately. Domain/hardware exit terms are visible before those separate purchases. Support can retrieve agreement version and evidence without seeing unrelated sensitive data. A staff member's shareable profile never obscures who the customer contracts with.
