# 75 · Privacy, marketing, safety and data operations

Their supplier, consent, review/report and publication decisions supersede earlier open-ended wording. Read the explicit evidence and legal readiness limits.

Edition 1.0 · Operational design for UK-first review; exact obligations require the actual processing/activity assessment.

## Purpose-by-purpose allocation

Map merchant customer/work records, Taplo customer accounts, discovery/personalisation, payments/fraud, support, platform reporting, marketing and employment-related access separately. A contract labelling Taplo “processor for everything” cannot replace analysis of who determines purposes/means. ICO guidance sets requirements where controller–processor processing applies [R23]. Define the applicable role and legal basis per purpose, recipients, source, location, retention trigger and rights route.

Merchant processing terms/DPA, customer privacy notice, merchant privacy information and employee/participant notices must reflect the actual roles. Record subprocessors, contractual authorisation/change notices, international transfer mechanism/assessment where needed, security/assistance and return/deletion obligations. No vendor is approved merely because an API exists.

## Data inventory and limits

Identity/contact verification; merchant/staff membership; customer/participant relationship; address/coverage; work/agreement; payment references; messages/assets; reward/pass; hardware claim; domain registrant; analytics; support/audit. Collect sensitive learner/health/support information only for a defined supported purpose and access policy. Never place such details in public profiles, notification previews, SEO, generic analytics or support logs. Guest checkout is not permission to create unlimited cross-business customer profiles.

Define retention by record purpose/event and applicable requirement. Financial records, abandoned drafts, failed uploads, marketing evidence, support material, logs and backups need different schedules. Record owner, deletion/anonymisation process, legal hold, provider propagation and verification evidence. No arbitrary retention duration is imposed in this pack. A deletion UI explains retained records and ongoing commitments honestly; account deletion does not erase other people's lawful transaction evidence.

## Rights and incident workflows

Request → verify identity/scope proportionately → identify relevant roles/records/providers → track legal response deadline → review disclosure/redaction → deliver securely → action deletion/correction/restriction as applicable → record outcome. Do not disclose another person's data in an export. Handle guardian/participant authority specifically. Security incident intake preserves evidence, contains exposure, assigns lead and assesses required regulator/individual/provider notifications against current rules; deadlines and responsible contacts must be configured before launch. No promise that every incident follows one generic notification template.

## Marketing and tracking

Transactional messages serve the booking/order/account task; adding promotions can change their classification. ICO's current electronic-mail guidance distinguishes consent and conditional soft-opt-in routes [R26]. Store sender, channel/contact, purpose, notice/consent evidence, timestamp and withdrawal/suppression. Buying/saving/joining a team is not blanket permission for Taplo and every merchant to market. A merchant's existing consent cannot be assumed transferable across businesses.

Inventory cookies/SDK/local storage by purpose and applicable rules. Do not render all tracking before a user choice where choice is required. Necessary transactional storage and optional analytics/personalisation/advertising have distinct treatment. Settings and withdrawal must work; avoid vague “accept everything to book” gates. Suppression must survive ordinary contact import; reimporting a list does not reset opt-out. Email/SMS/WhatsApp costs and templates follow channel/provider terms.

## Children, content and trust

ICO explains that its Children's Code can apply to services likely to be accessed by children, not just services marketed at them [R9]. Assess access, profiling/geolocation, defaults and appropriate safeguards for Taplo's actual customer service. Do not assume an adult payer removes every child-related concern. Guardian permission, ability to see lesson details and consent to public photos are separate issues.

For reviews/profiles/galleries/messages, document illegal/abusive/infringing content policy, reporting, triage, evidence, restrictions, appeal and review. Assess Online Safety Act scope using current Ofcom materials [R31]; if duties apply, assign accountable lead and implement required assessments/measures. Also implement honest review solicitation/display/moderation under applicable consumer rules [R25]. Do not silently hide legitimate negative reviews or let merchants buy false verification badges.

## Evidence required before relevant capability launch

Approved purpose/role inventory and notices; DPA/subprocessor arrangements; implemented field/access boundaries; tested rights and incident flows; consent/suppression behaviour; retention/export/deletion jobs; applicable child/safety assessments; moderation case flow; exact public wording. This work belongs in the operating model and data design, not only a footer link.
