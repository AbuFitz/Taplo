# 98 · Business proposition, distribution and learning plan

Working edition 1.7 · 3 November 2026 · Adopted working business hypotheses, not verified market demand, final pricing or a launch promise.

## What the business buys

Working proposition: Your business, easy to choose. Easy to come back to. Taplo gives the owner a distinctive compact place to share, useful customer actions, and a calmer workspace for what happens next. An existing website remains valuable; Taplo can handle its booking/action/return layer. A merchant without a website gets the same operational capability through its mini-space.

The owner should feel attachment to the page and confidence in the work behind it. Appearance brings identity; correct scheduling, clear money and easy return create everyday value. Explore adds an optional way to be found as real supply/demand develops. No early value proposition should depend on a giant marketplace already existing.

## First reference cohort and exclusions

Focus initial validation on independent repeat-service operators with straightforward appointments and optional small retail catalogue. Use barber/service-plus-retail as the main reference, with mobile quote work and maker/workshop tasks as stress comparisons. This is a research/delivery focus, not a permanent industry restriction. Do not imply clinical records, vehicle finance, complex restaurant operations or every creator-commerce model is ready because broad category labels exist.

Investigate their recent real work: how a new customer arrived, how time was agreed, what went wrong, where price/payment was recorded, and what makes customers return. Compare actual current tools and total useful cost rather than asking whether they like the Taplo concept. No completed interviews or paid commitments are claimed in this edition.

## Adoption ladder

First offer: an attractive useful business link/action alongside what they already use. Next: one deliberately adopted operational workflow with a clear source of truth. Then: team and customer continuity, followed by rewards/physical sharing/discovery where relevant. Do not force an immediate full migration; equally, do not promise two independent calendars or stock systems stay synchronised automatically.

Setup assistance and honest migration boundaries matter. Explain which appointments/customer facts must be entered or reconciled, what cannot transfer (such as unsupported payment mandates/marketing authority), how existing commitments stay protected and what happens if the merchant leaves.

## Acquisition experiments to prepare

| Channel | Concrete experiment | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder-led local outreach | Demonstrate one business-relevant connected task to a small reachable cohort | Qualified interest, setup/use, objections, assistance and follow-up outcome |
| Existing website action | Install a deliberate link only with merchant authorisation | Eligible task completion and returning use, not raw clicks alone |
| Staff sharing | Professional-specific merchant link in permitted staff social context | Correct assignment, useful booking and attribution without customer privacy loss |
| Counter/physical touchpoint | QR/NFC for an actual relevant next action | Scan-to-use, comprehension, fulfilment/support costs; no forced purchase |
| Merchant referrals | Ask satisfied actual users to introduce a relevant peer | Genuine activation/retention and reward economics if offered |

This prepares experiments; it does not authorise contacting people, installing links on their sites or sending campaigns in this blueprint task. Reachable cohort size and spend are to be set from actual founder capacity; no fabricated acquisition budget or success rate.

## Activation and retention definitions

Owner setup activation: real authored identity plus at least one correct published eligible customer action. Operational activation: actual relevant transaction/task completed and managed correctly. Keep these separate so a beautiful but unused page is not called an active operating account. Customer value: completes and later finds/manages the task without unnecessary installation/account friction.

Track merchant cohorts by adopted capability and acquisition source, useful weekly/monthly operation where appropriate, repeat customer tasks, correction/support burden and churn reasons. Distinguish seasonality, merchant closure, involuntary billing churn and dissatisfaction. No universal weekly engagement target should misclassify an occasional workshop host as failing.

## Commercial working model

Keep predictable software access with explicit cost-bearing extras/allowances where warranted; final tiers/prices remain unset until verified costs and willingness-to-pay evidence exist. Separate SaaS contribution from hardware/domain contribution. Include support minutes, onboarding, messaging, storage/egress, monitoring, provider/account costs borne, fraud/dispute exposure and taxes where applicable. Trial/annual billing do not magically fix negative contribution.

Commercial proof sheet requires actual supplier price/source/date/currency, merchant usage scenarios, net revenue, contribution, support load, acquisition cost/payback and cash timing. Unknowns remain unknown rather than zero. Compare low-volume and heavy/support-heavy merchants and test whether allowance boundaries preserve existing commitments. No unsolicited surprise overage or paywall blocking customer receipts.

## Operating responsibility before launch

Assign accountable owners for product, engineering/security, merchant support, financial exceptions, privacy/rights, content reports and provider/hardware/domain incidents. One person may cover several roles only if the capacity/escalation is credible. Define coverage hours/response targets from actual staffing and publish only promises that can be met. Exercise a payment mismatch, account lockout, missing reminder, wrong stock, lost domain and merchant closure scenario before representing those operations as dependable.

## Decision gates

Continue when operators can set up/use the workflow, customers can complete/manage it and the operating/cost burden looks viable. Improve when a specific obstacle is identified; change the hypothesis when people prefer the current tool or the perceived value does not justify adoption. Stop unsupported/high-risk scope until obligations can be met. Do not invent numeric thresholds before selecting the cohort, task and observation method; agree them before each actual experiment so results are not rationalised afterwards.

The platform's emotional promise is reduced friction and a business space worth sharing. Its commercial proof is repeated useful work at a viable cost. Both are necessary; neither is established by the breadth of the blueprint alone.
