# Trust, reviews and marketplace safety

Current continuation · 2.4: CUSTOMER-WEB-AND-ACQUISITION, COMMUNICATIONS-AND-MARKETING, TRUST-REVIEWS-AND-REPORTING and LEGAL-PACK-INDEX deepen these rules. Their supplier, consent, review/report and publication decisions supersede earlier open-ended wording. Read the explicit evidence and legal readiness limits.

Status: proposed platform rules.

Business publication, payment eligibility and Explore eligibility are related but distinct. Record verification scope accurately: verifying a payment account does not prove every professional qualification or the quality of the merchant's work. Regulated categories need explicit onboarding and policy decisions before promotion.

Reviews should attach to a qualifying completed interaction where possible, with rules for invitations, edits, responses, reporting and appeal. Explain what “verified” means. Rewarding a favourable review is inappropriate; any review incentive requires policy and regulatory assessment. Do not remove criticism merely because a business pays for a plan.

Report flows cover misleading content, prohibited goods/services, impersonation, abusive messaging, safety concerns and intellectual-property complaints. Capture enough context, acknowledge receipt, triage by harm and preserve an appeal path. Suppression may apply to a single offer, page, discovery eligibility or full account; don't use one destructive global toggle for every issue.

Fraud patterns include fake bookings for rewards, card testing, return abuse, counterfeit accessories and account takeover. Controls should use proportionate signals, rate limits and human review where necessary. Avoid explaining exploitable detection thresholds publicly.

Acceptance: a moderated offer leaves existing customers access to legitimate receipts and support where appropriate. Merchant appeal does not expose reporter identity unnecessarily. Review invitation rules apply consistently across businesses. A user can block abusive communication without losing their own transaction record. Trust decisions have reason codes, evidence access controls and auditable reversals.
