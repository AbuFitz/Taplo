## Recommendation

Use Free, Core £12/month, Pro £24/month and Business £49/month as clean initial test anchors. Model the headline as inclusive of applicable VAT rather than advertise a deceptively low total; tax registration/supply treatment needs actual accounting review. Price publication remains gated by cost, supported-feature readiness and actual willingness-to-pay evidence. Do not claim these are mathematically optimal or universally the cheapest.

## Free: deliberately attractive and bounded

Allow an owner to create/share a genuine page and receive/manage real business, including basic deposit protection once eligible. Five active offers can be any supported basic offer types; archived history does not consume the live-offer allowance. Basic manual quote/enquiry remains available; complex automated milestones/series/credit models can be paid capability layers. Do not restrict public contact or force card details just to try Free.

The 30-commitment allowance is a proposed product/economics experiment, not a sourced market truth. Define a stable commitment identity so the same purchase represented by related booking/order/payment records counts once. A deliberately confirmed booking, accepted quote that creates a commitment, or accepted goods order consumes one; edit/reschedule/payment retries do not. Group/session quantity and compound workflows require an explicit metering fixture before enabling them; do not invent a rule at billing time.

Warn at a proposed 80% threshold, show exact counter/period/reset date and offer upgrade before accepting an out-of-allowance new transaction. Existing work, management, receipts, lawful refunds and necessary notifications continue. Ordinary enquiries/contact remain available; don't automatically transform an instant booking into a request without customer disclosure. Never cancel accepted work or auto-charge an upgrade because the allowance was reached. Anti-abuse handling must not encourage fabricated cancellations to reset counters.

The period is a disclosed monthly UTC usage cycle initially; local display shows reset clearly. The owner can remain on Free and use the genuine included value indefinitely under its terms. Taplo branding is restrained, never a gaudy penalty. Remove/minimise optional promotional attribution can be a paid appearance benefit; required seller/provider disclosures remain.

## Extras and no-surprise policy

SMS/WhatsApp, bulk campaigns, domain registration/renewal, physical goods, shipping and optional managed services have separately disclosed charges where offered. Connecting an owned domain is distinct from buying one. No mandatory customer booking surcharge or Taplo discovery commission in this proposed initial model; ordinary processor charges still apply. Verify economics before advertising that commercial promise.

Do not include hundreds of SMS in £12 merely to look generous. Twilio's current UK page lists $0.056 per common outbound segment before applicable extras; 500 one-segment sends is $28 in supplier base cost, not £28 and not all-in. Unicode/long messages can consume more segments. Sender/phone/failed-message/carrier costs and FX/tax affect the actual GBP price. [R77] Paid SMS credit prices remain unset until actual supplier configuration/quotes and margin are verified.

Operational email is included for valid accepted activity under defined supported usage and abuse limits. Promotional sends are a distinct metered service, even when campaign tools are included. Never quietly turn marketing overage into a card debit without agreed authority. Customer app/manage access remains free to the customer in this model.

## Current comparison findings

Booksy UK lists £40/month plus £5 per extra user, excluding VAT, with a substantial feature bundle and stated messaging allowances. Do not portray it as an empty paid calendar or claim Taplo wins by base price alone. [R73] Fresha's retrieved page charges by bookable team member but rendered CHF; this research does not establish a comparable GBP total. [R74]

Beacons current free offering includes commerce/growth functions and seller fees, with paid levels changing features/fees. Linktree also lists selling/growth functionality; its retrieved price values did not render, so no exact GBP price is claimed. Stan official help identifies $29/$99 subscriptions; SimplyBook official help documents a bounded useful free plan. [R75–R76, R79–R80] These are different scopes/currencies; no simplistic one-column cheaper-than-everyone ranking.

Taplo's working differentiation: compact custom business identity, useful native operations, optional website coexistence and private customer continuation, with included team access and clear extras. This is a hypothesis to demonstrate and test, not a proven unique moat.

## Unit economics and release gate

Use real net recognised revenue after applicable tax, not the advertised headline. Under an explicitly illustrative 20% tax stress assumption, £12/£24/£49 correspond to £10/£20/£40.83 before servicing costs; this assumption is not tax advice. The existing illustrative £13 ordinary-use cost would lose money on Core and leave £7 contribution on Pro under that scenario, before excluded costs. A £48 support-heavy month can lose money even on Business. Do not hide these stress cases to make attractive prices look validated.

Free acquisition cost includes hosting, email, storage and support. Model paid conversion and free-account cost together; do not assume free is nearly costless. Postmark's retrieved base pricing at its displayed 10,000-email volume starts at $15/month; merchant domain needs and platform model also matter. [R78] Real hosting/support/FX inputs remain unknown in the cost model.

Before publication: verify supplier/account arrangements and variable costs; choose manageable initial capabilities; define exact allowance/metering/downgrade rules; show actual merchant adoption value; run ordinary/heavy/free abuse scenarios; confirm support capacity and tax treatment. Adjust features/price if costs or customer evidence conflict. Start monthly; defer annual discounts/long-term promises until retention and operating costs are understood.

## Pricing confidence and communication refinement · 2.0

Founder challenges perceived price and separate communications costs. Clarification: ordinary booking/order confirmations and necessary operational emails are included; bulk promotional email and SMS/WhatsApp are separately costed, not every email. Campaign software inclusion is distinct from delivery volume.

The £12/£24/£49 anchors were selected as a clean entry/expanded/organisation ladder against current scope and competitor context. They were NOT derived from observed willingness to pay, verified Taplo cost data or an optimisation model. Illustrative support scenarios are stress tests, not supplier invoices. Do not defend the numbers as correct merely because they are documented, or promise customers will pay them.

Adopt Free plus one paid offer as the first pricing experiment; use Core £12 as its current test anchor, with exact supported inclusion/value made clear. Retain Pro £24/Business £49 only as modelling scenarios until those incremental needs and costs are evidenced, not an obligation to launch a four-column paywall. This is a launch-test simplification, not deletion of the broader feature plan or an automatic change to real billing.

Do not react by inventing a lower price and calling it validated. Evaluate adoption objections, useful completed work, retained use, direct commercial choices and actual cost. Test bundled SMS alternatives only with a deliberately costed allowance; no unlimited promise. If merchants do not perceive the useful value, revise product/proposition as well as price. No guarantee of paid demand is established.
