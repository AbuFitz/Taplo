# Taplo · Legal requirements become release controls

Working edition 2.5 · 3 November 2026. This register answers what is being addressed and what proves compliance. It is a scoped UK-first planning register, not an exhaustive legal opinion or certification. Nothing is marked met solely because a draft exists. Ten drafts and their unresolved fields remain in LEGAL-PACK-INDEX.

## The honest position

The blueprint has considered many relevant legal areas and specified safeguards. It has not established every applicable requirement or verified every control in a real product. Meeting them requires the actual entity, launch territories/activities, provider contracts/configuration, reviewed documents, implemented workflows and operational evidence. The correct goal is a traceable no-release-until-ready process, not a promise that planning itself covers all legal risk.

## Evidence states and authority

Use states: identified → applicability_assessed → specified → implemented → reviewed → verified_for_scope → monitoring. A requirement can need rework at any stage; scope/version/date matter. Record accountable product/engineering/operations owner and actual reviewer; an owner can be the same person in a small team, but independence/conflicts are recorded. Legal reviewer approval does not prove implementation; passing browser tests does not settle law.

Per control: requirement/source/effective date, applicable scope, feature/route/record, policy/document version, implementation evidence, test/rehearsal, professional decision where needed, operating owner and review trigger. A waiver cannot authorise unlawful conduct. Unknown applicability is a tracked question with a release decision, not silently compliant.

## Release policy

Block the relevant feature/category/territory if its material legal/authority/safety requirements are unresolved. Do not block an unrelated safe function needlessly or put development caveats into customer wording. A merchant's acceptance cannot remove Taplo's own duties. A footer link, Terms accepted checkbox or GDPR badge is not evidence of compliance.

Console shows affected capability and missing evidence to authorised staff; it does not let an AI agent mark a legal requirement met by summarising a document. Versioned approved controls feed implementation tasks, legal publication and monitoring. Publishing legal drafts with unresolved entity/schedule fields remains blocked. The company can plan broad coverage while releasing only genuinely supported workflows.
