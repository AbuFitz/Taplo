## Current ecosystem comparisons

- Wix Spaces member journey: https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-using-spaces-by-wix-as-a-member . Members can return to business sites and manage purchases, bookings and related activity. This is direct evidence that connected branded business/customer ecosystems already exist.
- Wix Spaces site management: https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-managing-your-sites-on-the-spaces-app . Study pin/hide/leave and site search, rather than copying every interaction.
- Shop saved products and followed stores: https://help.shop.app/en/shop/shopping/discover/save-products-and-follow-stores . Distinguish saved offers, followed businesses and private order history in Taplo.
- Shop order management: https://help.shop.app/en/shop/managing-orders . Customer order history, receipts and merchant help illustrate post-purchase usefulness.
- Shop browser experience: https://help.shop.app/en/shop/getting-started/using-shop-com . Customer web can carry useful discovery and account activity alongside an app; app/browser capability parity needs deliberate scope.
- Shopify Fall 2026: https://www.shopify.com/uk/editions/fall2026 . Compare against the current administration and customer ecosystem.
- Linktree: https://linktr.ee/features . Current lightweight pages already include more than links.
- Beacons release record: https://beacons.ai/i/whats-new . Compare current creator/store/editor tooling.
- Square UK appointments: https://squareup.com/gb/en/appointments/features . Study scheduling together with broader operations.

## Loyalty and physical sharing

- Square loyalty refunds: https://squareup.com/help/gb/en/article/6130-manage-your-square-loyalty-program . The effect of refunds varies by earning model and refund type; this motivates explicit Taplo ledger rules, not a claim that Taplo must copy every Square rule.
- Blinq accessory pairing, updated 10 August 2026: https://support.blinq.me/en/articles/71524-pairing-your-own-nfc-accessories . Modern profile sharing supports compatible writable accessories; Taplo's provisioning, permissions and destination design remain its own responsibility.
- Blinq current product: https://blinq.me/ . Do not compare only against an old static digital-card product. NFC sharing itself is not a defensible novelty claim.

## Payment, policy and accessibility

- Stripe Connect disputes: https://docs.stripe.com/connect/disputes . Charge arrangement and negative-balance responsibilities affect dispute handling and financial exposure. Taplo must choose the arrangement before assigning support authority.
- Stripe merchant of record: https://docs.stripe.com/connect/merchant-of-record . Seller/payment responsibilities require a deliberate model.
- Stripe evidence: https://docs.stripe.com/disputes/visual-evidence . Evidence must address the actual dispute reason and underlying transaction.
- UK online selling: https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses/online-selling . Relevant to total costs, payment commitment, correction, confirmation and digital-content design; this pack does not replace legal review.
- UK distance selling: https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses . Use with the chosen offer and jurisdiction, rather than assuming a single universal cancellation policy.
- WCAG 2.2: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ . Design target for the proposed interactions; no conformance certification is claimed.

## Evidence we still need

Independent owner/customer task observations, real setup time, owner willingness to pay, retention, support costs, discovery acquisition, payment/fulfilment economics, category-specific obligations and comparative task recordings. No user research or competitor end-to-end transactional testing is fabricated in this pack.

Further research should deepen one workflow at a time: trigger, required data, decision rules, state changes, money, permissions, failure recovery, customer communication and outcome. Recording another hundred feature names would not answer those questions.
