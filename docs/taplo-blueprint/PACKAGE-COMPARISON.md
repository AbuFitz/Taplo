## Limits without gotchas

Define a commitment as one accepted booking/reservation or placed order. A class group booking is one booking with explicit capacity/participants; each separately accepted recurring occurrence counts once. A quote/enquiry is not counted until it becomes a booking/order; separate enquiry anti-abuse limits cannot silently reduce the advertised work allowance. Retries, edits, rescheduling and payment/refund attempts never create new commitment counts. Cancelled accepted work still consumed the acceptance count; system-created duplicates are reversed. Mixed transactions need explicit identity mapping before release.

Offer = one publicly selectable service/product/class offering, not each product variant. A recurring series has one parent offer with distinct accepted occurrences. An enquiry-only block does not create fake unlimited booking stock. Free users see counts and advance warnings; at the limit keep existing work operable and drafts intact, explain next reset or deliberate upgrade. No automatic upgrade. Campaign allowance resets monthly to the account's documented cycle/timezone; usage display names that date.

Proposed upgrade: show exact charge and allowance change before confirmation; already-used marketing sends subtract from the new total, not a fresh duplicate allowance. Downgrade applies at next renewal, asks the owner which excess offers/location functionality to retain, and preserves accepted work, receipts and recovery. Never auto-delete offers or obligations. Exact billing/proration/tax/credit fixture verification still required.

## Helpful expandable answers

**Do I pay more for my team?** No. Individual team access is included. Your plan depends on the tools and locations you need.

**Do I need text messages?** No. Essential booking and order emails are included. If you want texts, buy 100 units for £9 or 500 for £39. Longer messages can use more than one unit; we show the count before sending. Automatic top-up is off unless you enable it.

**What is a marketing email send?** One email to one recipient. A campaign sent to 100 people uses 100 sends. Your essential booking and order emails do not use that allowance.

**Can I keep my website?** Yes. Link customers straight to a Taplo booking, product or business page. Core and above add custom-domain connection and supported embedding.

**Do you charge booking commission?** Taplo's proposed packages have no platform transaction commission. Your payment provider's processing fees still apply. Hardware, domain purchases and optional texts are separate purchases.

**What happens if I stop paying?** You can cancel renewal. We show what changes and when; existing bookings, orders and financial obligations still have a safe handling route. Data export and closure follow the published exit policy.

## Page composition and source of truth

Mobile: short introduction → four readable stacked cards → shared-features strip → category-relevant comparison groups → optional-text examples → fees/exit FAQ. Keep the full comparison reachable; do not silently hide exclusions in a plan finder. Desktop: four aligned cards and accessible grouped comparison; tooltips never contain the sole explanation. Sticky CTA must not obscure table content or keyboard/focus.

Optional category selector changes examples/labels, not price or entitlements. A tutor sees lessons/students; a retailer sees products/orders. Mixed businesses can see both. No capacity hidden behind a misleading category choice. The plan finder recommends the lowest package supporting chosen capabilities/locations, then separately shows expected SMS purchases. Save its inputs without forcing sign-up.

At implementation, pricing page, Studio upgrade screens, backend entitlement checks and support answers read one versioned catalogue. contracts/proposed-packages.json remains a proposal; release work must resolve each display row to an enforced feature ID, permitted actions, dependency, limit, failure and downgrade behaviour. No vague everything included claim.
