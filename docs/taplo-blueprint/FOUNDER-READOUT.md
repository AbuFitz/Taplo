## What is missing from the blueprint itself

1. **A reconciled build-ready first journey:** the plan has broad connected coverage but still needs one complete scenario with exact data/command/error contracts, all key screens/states and operational recovery linked together. A new chapter alone does not supply this.
2. **Uniform category depth:** tutoring, mobile repair, retail and workshops have deeper profiles; team/barber work has additional detail. Other specialist workflows and every category's edge states are not complete. Spec 91 is the completion map.
3. **Finished visual production:** full Studio redesign, contextual editors, private return view, actual maps, populated mobile/desktop/light/dark interactions, motion/asset/icon/font manifest and every important state need designed artefacts. Existing ten screen references and atlas are only part of this.
4. **Exact engineering reconciliation:** proposed payment authority/allocation, policy, notification and guidance structures still need typed schemas/commands, migrations, permissions and scenario fixtures. Current machine files do not cover all new prose automatically.
5. **Final integration contracts:** actual provider/account/risk settings, sender/country readiness, calendar scopes/freshness, domain/Wallet/NFC agreements and supplier costs remain unresolved where identified.
6. **Systematic current market research:** targeted primary-source findings exist, but the wider category/competitor/unique-product research backlog in 85 is not complete. Review complaints need sampling and context, not anecdotes treated as measured prevalence.
7. **Complete operating ownership:** final support staffing/targets, escalations, moderation/appeals, financial exception ownership, incident recovery, migration and merchant closure need actual named capacity and rehearsed procedures.

## What is missing to establish a viable business

**A first buyer and sharp adoption reason.** The ecosystem can stay broad; the first acquisition message should identify a particular operator and a specific job worth switching or adding software for. Working validation focus: an independent repeat-service operator, using a barber/small service-plus-retail reference, with mobile-service/quote and maker/workshop scenarios as comparators. This updates the earlier maker-first default as a research priority, not proof of demand or a final launch restriction.

**Evidence of willingness to adopt and pay.** Documented competitors and internal designs cannot establish that merchants will complete setup, trust payments and continue using Taplo. Prepare thoroughly, then observe real connected tasks and actual commercial choices. A free signup count is not equivalent to active or retained paying merchants.

**A distribution plan.** Define first reachable merchant cohort, founder-led outreach/demo process, onboarding help, referral mechanism and how adoption is measured. Start by winning useful direct business/customer journeys; Explore becomes more valuable as genuine supply/demand grows. Do not make early value depend on a large marketplace already existing.

**Retention and measurable value.** Specify activation as a correct useful live setup plus actual meaningful use, not choosing a colour. Measure returning operators/customers, task success, exceptions, support burden and why merchants leave. Define cohort/window/denominator before setting numerical targets; no fabricated conversion benchmark.

**Viable economics and cash.** Replace unknown supplier inputs with real evidence, account for support/messaging/domain/hardware and risk costs, then decide prices/allowances and runway. No fixed tiers or unlimited promises are approved by this readout. Spec 66's formulas exist; verified cost inputs and commercial evidence still do not.

**Trustworthy delivery and accountable operation.** Real provider/device evidence, security/access/recovery tests and applicable specialist review are required. Supply a service that keeps obligations during failure or merchant departure. No number of documentation pages establishes production reliability or legal clearance.
