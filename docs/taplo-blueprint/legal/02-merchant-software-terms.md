## Plans, charges and team access

The checkout identifies the plan, price, applicable taxes, billing period, renewal and any deliberate optional purchase. The current plan schedule describes limits and supported features. Ordinary team access does not carry a per-member fee. Payment-provider fees, domain registration, physical products/shipping and optional texts are separately identified where applicable.

Software renews on the schedule you accept until cancelled. You can cancel future renewal in billing settings or through support if access fails. Upgrade/downgrade review explains effective date, exact charge/credit and changes before confirmation. We do not automatically turn a Free limit into a paid subscription. ## Prospective changes to charges

We may propose changes to published charges to reflect material supplier/operating costs, legal requirements or an expressly described change to the service. The notice explains the amount or calculation, reason, affected services, effective date and your options. We provide at least 30 days' notice, or any longer period required by the applicable agreement or law, and a genuine opportunity to cancel future renewal and export your data before the change takes effect without a change-related penalty.

We do not retroactively change agreed charges for accepted customer work. A new transaction-fee charging basis requires deliberate business agreement before new affected work is accepted. Declining a proposed change does not remove safe handling of existing commitments, refunds or required records. Fixed/prepaid agreements and payment methods are treated under [APPROVED_PRICE_CHANGE_AND_TRANSACTION_FEE_SCHEDULE]. A published current 0% Taplo transaction fee is not a promise that every future new contract will use that rate; any change follows this process and the reviewed schedule.

## Payments for your goods and services

You are the identified seller for your sales. Connect an authorised payment account and complete the provider's verification/terms. Taplo does not promise escrow, guaranteed payout time, a chargeback win or that a deposit is always retainable. You authorise only the supported financial actions described and accepted in the payment/refund schedule. Staff finance authority is separately scoped. Do not use a new account to evade outstanding obligations.

Cancellation terms must be fair and applicable to the actual transaction. You remain responsible for lawful refunds/complaints and for funding authorised refunds where required. Taplo coordinates software faults, evidence and permitted processes; this does not make every commercial dispute a Taplo decision. Provider disputes and statutory rights remain available.

## Communications and customer information

Only send messages with a lawful basis and required channel permission. Consent to your marketing is not consent to Taplo or another business. Do not upload purchased/scraped lists, override suppression, disguise promotions as operational updates or exchange review rewards for favourable content. You approve enabled automations and permitted campaign spending. Provider acceptance/delivery does not prove reading.

The DPA applies where Taplo processes personal data on your documented instructions. Our Privacy Notice explains independent platform purposes. Do not upload sensitive/child records beyond an expressly supported workflow. You must ensure team access, customer privacy information and content rights are appropriate.

You retain your content rights and grant a limited service/moderation licence. Do not upload unlicensed media or customer/staff portfolios without authority. Our Acceptable Use and Reviews policies apply equally across packages. Suspension can be limited to content, discovery, new transactions or access according to the issue; notice and appeal follow the published policy where appropriate.

Published support arrangements specify channels, hours and priorities; no 24-hour guarantee exists unless separately agreed. Existing commitments, refund responsibilities and lawful record retention survive ordinary account closure. Export/transition/deletion and domain/NFC behaviour follow [APPROVED_EXIT_SCHEDULE].
