# Taplo products, domains and service exit schedules

Working legal draft · edition 2.4 · 3 November 2026. Not approved for publication. Bracketed fields and linked schedules must be completed from actual facts; qualified legal review and implemented controls are required. The following is proposed customer-facing text, not development-status copy for the product.

## Taplo accessories

Where Taplo is seller, your order identifies [TAPLO_SELLER_DETAILS], product/specification/compatibility, customisation, total taxes/shipping, delivery timing and support contact. An NFC accessory directs compatible devices to the stated managed destination. We do not promise every phone supports every NFC-writing feature. Business account identity and permission are checked before claim/reassignment.

[APPROVED_HARDWARE_SAFETY_CONFORMITY_RETURNS_AND_WARRANTY_SCHEDULE] describes supplier evidence, applicable marking/labelling, batch traceability, faults, lawful consumer cancellation/returns and any narrowly applicable personalised-goods exception. Do not label the whole range non-returnable. NFC destination services, replacement and revocation have the clearly disclosed service duration and post-subscription behaviour below; no undefined lifetime claim.

## Domain connection and purchasing

Connecting a domain you already hold authorises only the stated routing/verification work. Keep your registrar access; website routing does not include email hosting or grant permission to overwrite existing MX/email records. We verify domain control and remove routing safely on exit. DNS propagation/security limitations are explained without pretending every change is instant.

Do not enable in-Studio purchase until [APPROVED_REGISTRAR_RESELLER_SCHEDULE] identifies registrar, registrant rights, initial/renewal/transfer fees, duration, contact verification, renewal choice/notice, redemption/grace, disputes, transfer-out and applicable cancellation/refund restrictions. Domain purchase/renewal is a separate deliberate order, not an unexplained subscription charge. The merchant must receive appropriate control and exit rights; Taplo does not claim to own every merchant's name.

## Ending software service

Cancellation of software renewal does not cancel existing customer appointments/orders, discharge lawful refunds or erase provider disputes. Before downgrade/closure, show the effective date, retained capabilities, excess offers/locations and export choices. [APPROVED_EXIT_TIMINGS_AND_FORMATS] defines a realistic transition/export period and required retention/deletion/backup treatment, with no premium gate for mandatory data rights.

[APPROVED_NFC_STABLE_LINK_CONTINUITY] must state what an already-sold card's link does when paid software ends: the retained basic destination, owner-managed redirect or other disclosed outcome. Do not sell accessories until this duration/cost obligation is resolved. Domain renewal/transfer obligations and financial evidence have separate schedules; one account-close button cannot silently delete them.
