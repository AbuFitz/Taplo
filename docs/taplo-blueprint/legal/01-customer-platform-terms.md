## Businesses and purchases

Businesses on Taplo sell their own goods and services. The seller's identity, offer details, total price, payment schedule and relevant cancellation/fulfilment terms are shown before you commit. A staff member's profile does not by itself make that person the seller. Your purchase agreement is with the identified seller unless the offer expressly identifies Taplo as seller, such as our own accessories.

We provide the platform and remain responsible for our own services and legal obligations. Describing the seller's responsibilities does not remove your rights against Taplo where they apply. Payment processing is supplied through the provider identified in checkout; refunds and provider/card disputes have separate processes.

## Accounts and access

Provide accurate information and keep account access secure. Only act for another person when authorised to do so. Guest links give access to the stated transaction, not another person's account. Tell us promptly if access may be compromised. We may require proportionate checks before disclosing private records or changing account ownership.

## Bookings, orders and changes

Review details and correct errors before confirming. A submitted request is not accepted until the stated acceptance conditions are met. Payment, booking confirmation and payout are different events. We provide access to the applicable agreement and updates in a form you can retain.

You can use the available management controls to request changes or cancel. The displayed accepted terms and applicable law govern any money outcome. A refund marked processing has not necessarily reached your bank. Ask the seller about delivery/performance; contact Taplo about platform faults, access or unresolved reporting/escalation. These routes do not prevent exercising statutory or provider rights.

## Reviews, content and messages

Use Taplo honestly and follow our Acceptable Use, Reviews and Reporting policies. You retain rights in content you own and grant the limited permission needed to store, display and process it to provide/moderate Taplo. Do not upload material you have no right to share, impersonate another person or publish someone else's private information. We do not require a positive review as a condition of a refund or support.

We may restrict relevant content or features to address policy breaches, safety, fraud or legal requirements. Where appropriate we explain the reason and appeal route; urgent lawful safety action may precede notice. We aim to preserve appropriate access to existing records and support.

## Preferences and ending use

Marketing choices are separate from account terms and necessary transaction updates. Manage preferences or unsubscribe without cancelling a legitimate order. You may close your account through Account or contact support. We explain what is deleted, what remains for lawful reasons and how accepted commitments can still be handled.
