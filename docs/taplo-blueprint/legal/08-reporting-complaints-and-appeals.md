# Reporting, complaints and appeals

Working legal draft · edition 2.4 · 3 November 2026. Not approved for publication. Bracketed fields and linked schedules must be completed from actual facts; qualified legal review and implemented controls are required. The following is proposed customer-facing text, not development-status copy for the product.

## Choose the right route

Use Report on a business, offer, review or message to flag suspected scams, prohibited/unsafe content, impersonation, abuse, privacy issues, intellectual-property infringement or review manipulation. You can also use [PUBLIC_REPORT_URL] without installing the app or buying anything. Account/service complaints go to [SUPPORT_ROUTE]; privacy requests go to [PRIVACY_ROUTE]. These routes connect where a case overlaps.

Tell us the relevant URL/item, what concerns you and safe relevant context. Do not send bank/card secrets, unrelated private documents or copies of suspected illegal imagery. An optional contact lets us ask questions/update you. Anonymous reporting and confidential identified reporting are different; we explain the available follow-up. Taplo is not an emergency service; use appropriate emergency services for imminent danger.

## What happens next

We acknowledge accepted reports with a reference where suitable, triage risk, review relevant evidence and assign responsibility. Our published support coverage and [APPROVED_REVIEW_TARGETS] describe expected updates; legal/safety obligations take priority. A receipt means the report was received, not that we have decided against the business. We may combine duplicate cases while retaining relevant evidence.

Actions can affect one item, publication, discovery, messages, new transactions or an account. We aim to preserve safe access to legitimate transaction records/support. Reporter identity is not routinely disclosed to the reported business, but absolute anonymity cannot be promised where law or a fair/legal process requires relevant disclosure. We explain material limits where possible.

## Financial and commercial issues

The identified merchant normally handles the goods/service and its lawful refund duties. Taplo handles platform faults, account/billing issues and process coordination. Reporting does not automatically cancel an order, release a deposit or suspend statutory/provider rights. Card-provider disputes follow their own process and deadlines; avoid duplicate reimbursement and contact the appropriate support route when processes overlap.

## Challenging a decision

A decision notice explains the affected material/action, relevant policy, shareable reasons and how to appeal. Submit relevant facts or explain the error through [APPEAL_ROUTE] with the case reference. We review with appropriate separation from the original decision where practicable, explain the outcome and correct restrictions/public information when appropriate. We do not guarantee every appeal succeeds or demand review deletion to provide support.

Good-faith reporting is welcome. Deliberate abusive reporting may lead to proportionate limits without removing genuine legal/safety reporting routes.
