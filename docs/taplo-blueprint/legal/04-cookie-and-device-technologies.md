## What this notice covers

We explain cookies, local storage, mobile SDKs, pixels and other technologies that store or access information on your device. This applies to the Taplo surfaces listed in [ACTUAL_DEVICE_TECHNOLOGY_INVENTORY], not just the marketing homepage. Contact [PRIVACY_CONTACT] with questions.

## Our choices

We use necessary technologies to maintain secure sessions, protect the service, keep an active basket/task and remember a privacy choice where the relevant exception applies. We do not assume a technology is necessary merely because a supplier includes it. Optional analytics, advertising, tracking or personalisation is enabled only under the applicable lawful conditions and your relevant choice.

The inventory identifies the technology/provider, purpose, duration, first/third-party access, applicable exception or consent requirement, affected domains/apps and how to change the choice. We assess multi-purpose use carefully; a statistical/appearance exception cannot be extended to advertising because the same SDK also performs analytics. Device permission, marketing permission and tracking permission are different choices.

## Managing preferences

Use Privacy choices from the footer or Account settings to review/change optional choices. Where consent is required, declining is as accessible as accepting and optional technologies remain off until a valid choice. Withdrawal stops future optional use and triggers appropriate removal/provider handling. Core booking, buying, reporting and legal access remain available when you decline optional tracking.

Browser/app controls can also affect storage and permissions. Clearing storage may remove preferences and require a fresh choice; we do not fingerprint you to recover them. Merchant custom hosts and external sites may have different controllers/technologies, which are identified at the relevant boundary rather than covered by an unexplained global acceptance.
