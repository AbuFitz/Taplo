# Reviews policy

Working legal draft · edition 2.4 · 3 November 2026. Not approved for publication. Bracketed fields and linked schedules must be completed from actual facts; qualified legal review and implemented controls are required. The following is proposed customer-facing text, not development-status copy for the product.

## Genuine experience, clear evidence

Taplo reviews must describe a genuine experience with the identified business. We check eligibility using relevant transaction or other evidence. A label such as Booked through Taplo describes that evidence; it does not guarantee every statement or the quality of the service. A cancelled or disputed interaction may still be a genuine experience and is not automatically excluded.

Write honestly in your own words. Do not impersonate customers, post fake or conflicted independent reviews, coordinate manipulation or disclose other people's private information. We do not permit incentives for posting, changing or removing a review. Businesses must not pressure customers for positive ratings or condition refunds/support on changing a review.

## Invitations and publication

Invitations are neutral and follow the applicable communications rules. We do not ask only satisfied customers to review publicly or route negative feedback away from the review process. Businesses may reply or report a policy concern; they cannot approve/veto their own reviews because they dislike them or pay for a plan.

We screen submitted reviews for eligibility and policy issues. Some require investigation before publication. We may ask for proportionate private evidence, redact prohibited personal information or remove content under the policy while preserving honest criticism where possible. A policy hold/decision and relevant appeal route are communicated appropriately. Reviewing a report is not proof an allegation is true.

## Ratings and external sources

Taplo's displayed native rating/count is calculated from currently published eligible Taplo reviews under [PUBLISHED_AGGREGATION_AND_SORT_RULES]. Editing one review does not count it twice. The review view explains available sorting and material exclusions/limitations. Handpicked testimonials are not presented as a complete review history.

Google reviews remain clearly separate and subject to Google's policies. A link to Google does not mean the reviews were imported, verified by Taplo or combined into the Taplo score. We do not automatically post a customer's review on another service.

## Reports and appeals

Use Report beside a review/reply or the public Reporting route. We assess relevant evidence, record the reason and take proportionate action. Authors and affected businesses can challenge moderation through the stated appeal process. Payment/refund disputes are separate; a review is not a substitute for a financial claim. We regularly assess fake-review risks and the effectiveness of our controls; outsourcing delivery/display does not remove our publishing responsibilities.
