# Merchant customer agreement template

Working legal draft · edition 2.4 · 3 November 2026. Not approved for publication. Bracketed fields and linked schedules must be completed from actual facts; qualified legal review and implemented controls are required. The following is proposed customer-facing text, not development-status copy for the product.

## Seller and your purchase

Your seller is [MERCHANT_LEGAL_NAME], trading as [DISPLAY_NAME], at [LEGAL_CONTACT_ADDRESS], contact [CUSTOMER_CONTACT], [REGISTRATION_VAT_OR_PROFESSIONAL_DETAILS_WHERE_REQUIRED]. Taplo provides the platform; the seller supplies the described goods/service unless the offer states otherwise.

Your agreement summary identifies [OFFER_DESCRIPTION], chosen variant/service scope, quantity/participants, date/time/timezone or delivery/collection window, location/service area, total price including applicable taxes and delivery, payment now, later payment and any minimum term. Review/correct these details before confirming. Optional extras require your choice and are not added automatically. We provide the accepted summary and terms in a form you can save.

## Choose the applicable supply branch

**Appointment/service:** [SERVICE_ACCEPTANCE_AND_SCOPE] explains when the booking is accepted, what is included and prerequisites. [RESCHEDULING_CUTOFF_AND_REVIEWED_CANCELLATION_OUTCOME] explains changes, cancellation, deposits and any justified fee. Deposit is part-payment towards the total. Any retention must meet applicable rights and fair terms; no automatic double recovery. Where a distance-contract cancellation right applies, [REVIEWED_SERVICE_CANCELLATION_NOTICE] and any lawful express early-performance request are separately presented. A selected date alone does not make every service a statutory exception.

**Physical goods:** [DISPATCH_COLLECTION_DELIVERY_AND_RISK] identifies supported territories, timing/cost and who arranges return. [GOODS_CANCELLATION_AND_FAULTY_GOODS_NOTICE] describes applicable cancellation/return rights, the correct refund route and any lawful exception only for the actual goods. A customised/perishable exception cannot be applied to all stock. Statutory remedies for faulty/misdescribed goods remain separate from change-of-mind returns.

**Class/workshop:** [PARTICIPANT_CAPACITY_REQUIREMENTS] explains seats, accessibility/joining and permitted substitutions. [REVIEWED_DATED_ACTIVITY_RIGHTS] determines whether a specific statutory cancellation exception applies; it is not assumed for all tutoring/classes. Explain organiser cancellation and refund/voluntary alternatives.

**Digital content/membership:** Do not publish until [DIGITAL_OR_RECURRING_SUPPLY_SCHEDULE] covers actual supply, term/renewal, lawful cancellation, express consent/acknowledgements where needed and durable confirmation. A generic terms checkbox is not a substitute for required specific acknowledgement.

## If something changes or goes wrong

If we cannot supply as agreed, we explain the options and provide refunds/remedies required by law. You do not have to accept credit instead of a refund owed. Contact [MERCHANT_COMPLAINT_ROUTE]; Taplo support can help with platform/payment-status access and escalation. Pending refund and completed refund are different states. Payment-provider rights remain available.

[REVIEWED_SELLER_LIABILITY_JURISDICTION_AND_COMPLAINTS_TERMS] preserve mandatory consumer rights. The seller's privacy notice explains its customer/participant processing. Marketing choices are optional and separate. This template is generated only from completed reviewed merchant facts; missing required facts block publication/checkout rather than silently filled legal fiction.
