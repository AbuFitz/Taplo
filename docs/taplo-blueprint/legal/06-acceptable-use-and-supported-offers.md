# Acceptable use and supported offers

Working legal draft · edition 2.4 · 3 November 2026. Not approved for publication. Bracketed fields and linked schedules must be completed from actual facts; qualified legal review and implemented controls are required. The following is proposed customer-facing text, not development-status copy for the product.

## Use Taplo honestly and lawfully

Do not impersonate a business or person, misrepresent credentials/availability/prices, publish stolen content, expose private personal data, harass/threaten others, manipulate reviews/rewards or use Taplo for scams, phishing, malware, card testing or unlawful transactions. Do not bypass access limits, provider restrictions, safety controls or another person's consent.

Do not publish or trade illegal goods/services, child sexual exploitation/abuse material, terrorism content, unlawful hate/threats or other prohibited content. Report suspected harmful material through the contextual report route without downloading or forwarding illegal imagery. Taplo is not an emergency response service.

## Supported categories and claims

Offers must match a capability/category Taplo actually supports and applicable provider restrictions. Regulated, age-restricted or otherwise high-risk activities require the specific authorisation and controls in [APPROVED_CATEGORY_SCHEDULE] before publication/transactions. Absence from a short example list does not imply permission. We explain a restriction or safe alternative where appropriate.

You must hold relevant rights/permissions for logos, photos, portfolios, customer/staff images and descriptions. Decorative stock imagery must not be presented as evidence of your own work. Do not use a payment-account check to imply professional licensing, insurance or platform endorsement.

## Messages and reviews

Only contact people under the relevant purpose, permission and channel rules. Do not upload bought/scraped marketing lists, disguise promotional content as service updates or repeatedly contact someone who has opted out. Do not pay/reward for reviews, suppress criticism, fabricate transactions to earn credibility or require review removal for a refund.

## Investigation and enforcement

We use proportionate publication checks, reports and risk signals and may request relevant evidence. We can restrict the affected content, offer, discovery, messaging, new transactions or account according to the issue and lawful requirements. Urgent credible harm can require immediate protective action. We explain decisions and appeal options where appropriate, without disclosing protected reporter/security information or material we cannot lawfully disclose.

Existing customer records and safe support/refund routes are preserved where appropriate; account suspension does not extinguish obligations. Deliberate evasion through new accounts/content may lead to further proportionate action. Our Reporting and Appeals policy explains how to challenge a decision or report an error.
