# Merchant data processing addendum

Working legal draft · edition 2.4 · 3 November 2026. Not approved for publication. Bracketed fields and linked schedules must be completed from actual facts; qualified legal review and implemented controls are required. The following is proposed customer-facing text, not development-status copy for the product.

## Scope and instructions

This addendum forms part of the merchant software agreement between [PARTIES]. It applies only when the merchant is controller and Taplo processes the specified personal data on its behalf. Independent Taplo/provider purposes are allocated separately in the reviewed role schedule; this addendum does not relabel them.

Schedule A must specify subject matter/duration, processing nature/purposes, data types, data subjects and controller rights/obligations. Proposed scope includes authorised booking/order/customer operations, communication on merchant instructions and support of those operations. Sensitive/child data is included only for an expressly supported reviewed workflow.

Taplo processes covered data only on documented lawful instructions, including international-transfer instructions, unless UK law requires otherwise. Where legally permitted Taplo informs the merchant of that requirement before processing. Taplo informs the merchant if it considers an instruction infringes applicable data protection law and addresses the conflict before continuing that instruction.

## Confidentiality and security

Persons authorised to process covered data are bound by confidentiality. Taplo implements the appropriate technical/organisational measures specified in Schedule B, considering the actual risk: tenant/field access, secure authentication, encryption as appropriate, secret handling, resilient backups/restoration and regular security assessment. A general security-policy link cannot replace the actual agreed measures or make unsupported certification claims.

## Subprocessors and transfers

[PRIOR_SPECIFIC_OR_GENERAL_WRITTEN_AUTHORISATION_MODEL] and Schedule C identify authorised subprocessors and material locations/functions. For general authorisation, Taplo gives [REVIEWED_NOTICE_PERIOD] advance notice of intended additions/replacements and an appropriate opportunity to object; [REVIEWED_OBJECTION_RESOLUTION] defines the outcome. Equivalent required data-protection obligations bind subprocessors, and Taplo remains responsible as applicable for their performance. Applicable international transfers use the identified lawful mechanism and assessment; a provider's brand name alone is not approval.

## Assistance and incidents

Taking account of processing and available information, Taplo assists the merchant with data-subject rights, security/breach duties, DPIAs and required prior consultation. Direct requests concerning merchant-controlled data are routed appropriately, with no unnecessary disclosure. Taplo notifies the merchant without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting covered data and supplies available details/updates needed for its obligations. Actual incident contacts and escalation processes are in Schedule D; the processor must not wait for a final investigation before making a required notification.

## End of processing and evidence

At the controller's choice, Taplo returns or deletes covered data at the end of the relevant service and deletes copies unless law requires retention. The export/deletion schedule defines formats, timing, backups, restrictions and evidence; ongoing lawful obligations and independent records are distinguished. Taplo makes necessary compliance information available and allows/contributes to audits, including inspections, under a reasonable reviewed process without frustrating statutory rights.

Required completed schedules: A processing particulars; B actual security/restore measures; C subprocessors/locations/transfers/notice; D assistance and incidents; E return/deletion/audit. These are substantive unresolved contract inputs, not optional appendices. ICO's Article 28 contract guidance is referenced in LEGAL-PACK-INDEX.
