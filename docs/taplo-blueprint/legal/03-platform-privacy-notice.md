## Information and purposes

We process account/contact details, authentication and security records, business/team relationships, submitted public content, transaction references, messages, preferences, technical usage and support/reporting evidence as relevant to the service used. We obtain this from you, the business you interact with, authorised account members, payment/delivery providers and your device as described in the completed processing schedule.

For merchant-managed appointment/order/customer records, the merchant may determine the purpose and Taplo may process on its instructions. That allocation is made by actual activity, not by labelling all data processor data. The business's own privacy information explains its purposes. Contact either appropriate route; we coordinate a request where necessary rather than ignoring it.

## Public and private information

Business pages and submitted public reviews are public as described before publication. Private appointments, orders, messages, intake, family permissions and customer activity are access-controlled; they are not public marketing material. Choose a public review name carefully and do not include other people's sensitive information. Public information may be accessible through search/share services. Removing it from Taplo does not guarantee immediate removal from every independent cache.
