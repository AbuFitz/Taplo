# Taplo · Reviews, reports and active trust operations

Working edition 2.4 · 3 November 2026. Deepens 23–25 and 74–76. Merchant listings, customer reviews, replies and in-platform messaging create real publishing/safety work. Draft legal text and risk assessments must correspond to operating controls; no claim that a policy page establishes compliance.

## Review decision: native Taplo plus optional Google route

Native Taplo reviews support Explore and the business mini-space. Launch Google support is an optional clearly labelled link to the actual business's Google review page, configured and checked by its owner. No scraping, screenshot-star count or automatic import into a Taplo rating. A later owned-GBP integration requires approved access, owner authorisation, policy-compliant temporary storage/display/attribution and removal/disconnection handling [T3]. Places API is a different product with its own restrictions [T4]; do not casually combine Google-derived mapped results with a Mapbox design.

Taplo and Google ratings remain separate with sources, counts and retrieval dates where applicable. Do not add them together or label an external rating verified by Taplo. If Google integration fails, hide unavailable external information or label its actual age as allowed, never keep an invented evergreen score. Google links can exist without forcing an API integration or treating a listed business as verified by Google.

Google prohibits incentives and selective positive review solicitation [T5]. Taplo default therefore bans rewards/discounts for writing, revising or removing reviews on either route. No ask five-star customers to post on Google while routing unhappy customers privately. A neutral review destination choice is not conditioned on sentiment. A refund/dispute outcome cannot require deleting a review. Neither internal nor external review posting is automated on the customer's behalf.

## Eligibility and honest verification

A Taplo review comes from a genuine identifiable interaction, with private verification but optional restrained public display name. A completed booking/fulfilled order gives transaction evidence. A cancelled, refused or disputed transaction can still represent a genuine customer experience; do not exclude criticism simply because the merchant never marked it complete. Route unfulfilled-experience reviews through an appropriate evidence path and label the scope accurately.

Proposed labels: Booked through Taplo; Ordered through Taplo; Experience checked. They describe evidence, not quality or full truth verification. No customer document becomes public. First release supports business/service/order experience reviews linked to the relevant record; a separate product-review ranking engine needs its own aggregation/deduplication contract. Do not automatically publish staff scoreboards from a business review.

One active review per reviewer/qualifying interaction; edits preserve history and update rather than duplicate score. Repeat genuine visits are eligible but suspicious pattern/rating impact is reviewed; detect fake transaction loops rather than outlawing regular customers. A shared family payer is not authority to impersonate each participant. No staff/owner/connected-party reviews misrepresented as independent customers. Imported merchant testimonials, if permitted later, are labelled testimonials and excluded from Taplo aggregates until equivalent verification rules exist.

## Creation and publication

Customer chooses rating, text and context; preview explains public display and what remains private. Default no public attachments in first release; text moderation and evidence uploads are separate. Do not collect unnecessary medical/learner detail. Allow a customer to report a private-data mistake and edit it. Review schema/AI summary must not fabricate sentiment or rewrite allegations as established fact.

States: draft → submitted → screening → published or held_for_review. A reviewer receives pending/published/decision state. Any hold has an internal reason and review owner. Merchant gets reply/report controls, not approval or veto over its own reviews. A policy dispute enters investigation; disagreement/low rating alone is not a reason to remove. Where only private details breach policy, offer proportionate redaction with record of changes rather than quietly changing the opinion.

Native score uses published eligible native reviews only; one review object contributes once after edits. Display count, rating distribution, review dates and default sort. Default newest relevant reviews, with clear alternative sort. Do not show only positives on the review destination or claim a handpicked showcase is the full history. If a review is temporarily excluded from a score, record why and explain material limitations. Removal/restoration reprojects counts/cache/structured data consistently. Search ranking and paid status cannot change the score calculation.

CMA guidance makes review publishers responsible for reasonable proportionate prevention/removal of fake or misleading review information, with published policy, risk assessment and detection/investigation/action processes [T1]. Those responsibilities also matter when using third-party review information; outsourcing does not erase them. Taplo must monitor false positives, review manipulation and material aggregate changes, not just profanity.

## Report anything relevant without a maze

Public business page menu/footer: **Report this business**. Offer popup, review, reply and conversation expose contextual **Report**. Customer account/help also has a public report form. No app install or purchase is required; unauthenticated reports accepted with anti-abuse controls. A contact address is optional unless the requested follow-up needs it; clearly distinguish anonymous submission from a confidential identified report. A private thread report requires legitimate access to the selected material.

Reasons: suspected scam/impersonation; prohibited or unsafe goods/services; misleading claim; harassment/hate/threat; child safety; privacy/personal data; intellectual property; fake/manipulated review; other. Booking refund question has a linked financial-case route; reporting does not automatically refund/cancel the transaction.

Auto-attach immutable target ID, public URL, content revision and relevant permitted context; ask for concise explanation and optional safe evidence. Reporter never selects another customer's private record. Do not request upload/copy of suspected child sexual abuse material or other illegal evidence; collect a location/reference via a restricted safety route instead. Warn against including unrelated sensitive personal details. Direct imminent-danger concerns to appropriate emergency services; Taplo is not an emergency response service.

Submission flow: select reason → context/details → review → submit → reference/status link when suitable. Network uncertainty preserves draft and idempotent reference rather than duplicating. Public receipt says report received, not business found guilty. Reporter visibility is limited; merchant may need relevant allegations to respond but is not automatically shown reporter identity/phone, internal fraud signals or other users' evidence.

## Detection before and after reports

Controls run on publication and material edits, relevant links, reviews/replies and payment-abuse signals. Examples: prohibited-category rules, malware/phishing URL checks, risky redirects, duplicate/impersonated merchant identity signals, unsafe claim patterns, suspicious review/transaction loops, spam and exposed personal details. Scan uploaded files for permitted type/size/malware before serving. Recheck published pages after link changes and risk signals; publication is not a one-time green tick.

Use deterministic high-confidence checks first; classifiers can prioritise review with versioned reasons. Do not promise a filter detects every unlawful act or use an unreviewed model verdict as irreversible guilt. Human review for ambiguous/contested decisions. Critical suspected harm may justify a reversible immediate hold before complete investigation under reviewed policy. Keep exact abuse thresholds private, record false positives, and provide appeal.

Apply purpose-limited access to private conversations. Do not give marketing/support unlimited access or claim all private messages are routinely read. Any automated/private-content analysis needs documented purpose, legal basis, notices, safeguards and actual risk assessment. Evidence handling may need restricted specialist access and lawful external reporting; no general employee downloads of harmful material.

## Console case and enforcement

Case fields: case/target ID and revision, reason/policy version, source report or detection, severity, evidence references/access class, current restriction, responsible queue/person, review-by target, decision/rationale, notifications and appeal linkage. Assignment and an acknowledgement do not count as resolution. Duplicate reports merge references without multiplying presumed guilt or erasing independent evidence.

Working queues: critical credible harm/repeated fraud requires on-call escalation; high suspected active scam/safety issue same staffed-day triage; ordinary policy/review issues target two staffed business days for first substantive review. These are proposed operational targets, not legal deadlines or promises to publish without staffing. Required regulatory response/removal obligations override ordinary targets. Resolve actual rota/cover before launch.

Restrictions are scoped: hold new publication; remove one offer/media/review; stop discovery; stop new transactions; restrict messaging; suspend account. Preserve safe access to existing receipts, legitimate bookings/refund/support where appropriate. Payment-provider reporting/restrictions are separate authority, not a magic Console freeze-payout button. Prevent evasion by recreating identical content/accounts without punishing legitimate ownership changes automatically.

Decision notice gives affected content, relevant policy, action, reasons to the extent safely/lawfully shareable, duration/next step and appeal route. Merchant/reviewer/reporter get appropriate different notices. Appeal assigned to a second reviewer where practicable; small team records conflict/independence limitations and escalation. Restoration updates discovery/assets/ratings/cache and notifies affected users appropriately. Delete public access separately from lawful evidence retention. Abusive reporting can be restricted proportionately, with genuine safety/legal routes preserved.

## Online safety and child-access scope

Ofcom's current checker expressly distinguishes reviews on provider content from reviews on third-party user-generated listings and in-platform messaging [T2]. Taplo should plan on potentially regulated user-to-user activity and obtain a recorded scope assessment; do not assume small size, B2B focus or calling Explore a directory creates an exemption. Search within Taplo is not automatically a separately regulated general search engine; assess the actual service boundary.

A child-access assessment is separate from a privacy Children's Code assessment. Tutoring/activities, child photos, shared family devices, messages and location need actual risk analysis. A 18+ terms clause alone is not evidence children cannot access. Applicable risk/children/records/reporting/complaints measures and accountable lead must be set before the relevant release; follow current Ofcom commencement/timing guidance rather than copy old historical deadlines [T6]. No default open child-to-stranger messaging.

## Trust governance and evidence

Maintain prohibited/restricted category policy with reasons and safe supported alternatives. No regulated-category promotion without its specific evidence/role/claims workflow. Account/payment verification badges state actual scope; no fake insured/professionally vetted badge. Trust is available on Free; paying cannot buy removal of criticism or reduced review scrutiny.

Track report volume/reason, action/appeal reversal, time to substantive review, repeat harm, fake-review patterns, detection precision samples and manipulated score impact. Logs and public transparency reporting need privacy/retention review. Model correctness, legal compliance and support capacity are separate release evidence.

Rehearse report without login, false report, active scam, one abusive review among valid ones, author edits while case open, malicious attachment, suspected illegal imagery reference, closed merchant with refund due, external-Google request, rating cache after removal, disputed cancellation review, staff appeal conflict and privacy deletion with evidence hold.

## Operating cost dependency

Content review, appeals, statutory assessments, legal/accounting work and incident cover consume real capacity. Existing support-minute averages are not evidence this trust workload is already funded. Measure cases, review time, specialist cost and escalation load separately; add/reconcile the resulting budget before relying on the company profitability estimate. Do not hide labour in a cheap cloud invoice or promise instant human review without cover. Headline prices remain the current proposal while that evidence is collected.
