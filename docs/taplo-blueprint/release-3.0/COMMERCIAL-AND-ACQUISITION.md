## Supported build/launch scope

Build one compact business mini-space, customer browser/app, owner Studio browser/app and protected web Console sharing canonical records. Core transaction capabilities: one-to-one service booking, physical goods/native stock/fulfilment, in-person dated classes/waitlist, scoped enquiries/quotes, optional lawful deposit/full/no-upfront payment, assigned team access, private customer return and scoped reviews/reporting. Pro adds specified repeat/credit/series/reward workflows and supported loyalty passes. All four packages are intended at launch; each advertised feature requires implemented readiness/authority and actual release evidence. Don't ship an empty higher plan or change the founder's all-plan intent to a single-paid-plan rollout.

Category support follows the thirteen operating profiles and enabled reviewed capabilities. Food/health/specialist regulated supply requires its stated actual category evidence before transaction enablement. Dealer presence/enquiry/viewings do not include vehicle-sale deposit/finance; clinics don't become a clinical record system. Online one-to-one, online group, recorded/digital and Studio software upgrades have their actual store/region/SKU purchase eligibility checked; physical outside-app work is not the same purchase type. No hidden browser checkout workaround or native software-upgrade CTA before supported policy.

Explicit non-inclusions: external-shop inventory/order synchronisation, public headless/arbitrary code, bespoke engineering in subscription, payroll/tax filing/clinical records, cross-merchant settlement/stored-value wallet, automatic no-show/card-on-file collection, route optimisation, guaranteed SEO rankings, unlimited file storage, free domain registration/mailbox or uncosted 24/7 support. In-Studio domain buying and dedicated NFC terminals remain separately contracted future products; domain connection and public QR destination are current included tools. These are controlled boundaries, not limits on later product evolution.

packages.json and package-features.json are canonical commercial tables for UI/API/support. A price/label/category/role title never grants authority. Free five parent live offers/30 new monthly commitments; product variants aren't separate offers. Paid no routine work-count cap doesn't mean unbounded provider spend/abuse. Marketing allowance per account cycle, not staff/location; operational notifications survive promotional exhaustion. Explicit optional SMS purchase, segment preview, one balance/history, auto-top-up off.

Upgrade quote shows exact tax/charge/proration/renewal and capability/allowance effect before confirmation; server quote bound to subscription version and actual price IDs. Downgrade/cancel next renewal with deliberate excess offer/location handling, accepted work/recovery/history/earned promises preserved. No automatic Free-to-paid upgrade or account deletion on invoice failure. Working grace: seven calendar days after payment failure for recovery, with continued accepted-work/refund/security access; don't advertise a contractual grace until actual billing terms/account treatment reviewed. Annual pricing is not offered in this baseline. Plan changes and future fees follow reviewed prospective notice/agreement/exit; no silent commission increase.

## Twelve-week preparation and learning programme

Weeks 1–2: complete main proposition, analytics purpose/consent inventory and first maker/workshop connected demo; prepare barbers and mobile-service solution stories. Weeks 3–4: complete category task examples, pricing comparison/fee FAQ and website-companion walkthrough; prepare authorised diagnostic recruitment. Weeks 5–6: observe small mixed owner/customer tasks and revise actual confusion; verify preview/share/search-index conditions in test build. Weeks 7–8: run a bounded authorised pilot, record activation/support/provider costs and actual plan choices. Weeks 9–10: publish only evidenced case studies/FAQs and improve content from real queries. Weeks 11–12: evaluate retained use, acquisition costs, cohort contribution and next supported category, not signup count alone. These are work stages, not promised launch dates or evidence already obtained.

Initial solution page packet per supported category: actual job/problem, three concrete workflow demonstrations, relevant team and customer return example, supported boundaries, page/Studio visuals, package starting point and plain FAQs. Homepage/solutions/features/pricing/app/sharing/help/legal routes link coherently. No automated outreach/advertising/messages were sent under this blueprint task.

## Measurement definitions

Activation = owner publishes a usable supported offer and successfully handles one eligible customer action in the observed period; not just account creation. Customer completion = correct intended commitment with accurate seller/amount/status understanding. Retention = returning to complete useful work over defined 30/60/90-day cohorts; no fake “active” from passive push. Acquisition = attributed actual cost and onboarding work per activated merchant, with consent/privacy-respecting instrumentation. Pricing evidence = deliberate real choice and continued use where authorised, distinct from polite survey agreement. Follow INTEGRATION-AND-OPERATING-EVIDENCE-PACK for actual observation; no real users/results are created by this schedule.
