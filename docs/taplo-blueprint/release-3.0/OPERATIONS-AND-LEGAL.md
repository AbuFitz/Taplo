# Operating and legal implementation schedules

Specification baseline 3.0. The ten existing legal drafts remain draft, with their publication register intact. This pack fills operational schedules and controls; it cannot supply an actual company address, signed provider contract, statutory applicability decision or professional approval from invented facts. external-facts.json and provider-schedules.json track those prerequisites independently from implementation. A preview/site or agent cannot publish uncompleted legal placeholders as approved terms.

## Responsibility and support

Working ownership: founder accountable for launch scope, vendor/entity facts, commercial decisions and operating coverage; engineering responsible for product/security/provider behaviour; trust/support responsible for case handling, evidence/privacy and escalation; actual legal/accounting specialist reviews scoped matters when required. Actual people/backup/hours must be recorded, not inferred as a hired team or 24/7 service. No change to the existing cost model is inferred from the disregarded pricing/support conversation.

Use help plus email/in-product case as launch support routes. Display actual hours/response expectation only after coverage exists. Every case has owner, category, urgency, next action, deadline basis, evidence scope and timeline. Triage priority: active security/access loss or current money incident; blocked accepted work; routine setup/how-to. Priority queue does not mean refund/dispute decisions automated or unlimited instant responses. Free users retain essential security/money/access/rights escalation. No one asks for passwords, OTPs, full cards or access to all customer data as a troubleshooting shortcut.

Console action policy: workforce sign-in+MFA, current case/action grants, fresh step-up for consequential actions, reason/evidence and audit; approvals for risky financial/ownership changes separate where staffed. Support can inspect safe operation projections/reconcile under authority, not silently impersonate the owner. AI can draft/classify/suggest, not invent provider outcomes or final legal/money decisions. Route merchant fulfilment/commercial disputes to the merchant first with fair escalation; Taplo owns its faults and platform duties. Chargeback/statutory routes aren't removed by a support policy.

## Privacy and retention

Use retention-schedule.json as proposed purpose-specific defaults, not an assertion each period is legally prescribed. Review actual entity/roles/activities/territories, legitimate purposes and minimisation before enabling. Financial proof is separate from full customer profile/intake. Retention job evaluates purpose/trigger/hold, deletes or anonymises eligible active data, tombstones public routes/CDN copies and queues provider deletion where supported. Individual rights require proportionate verification, correct controller/processor scope, redaction and applicable deadlines. Do not delete lawful financial proof to simulate account deletion or retain every attachment because some invoice must remain.

A legal hold records reason, actual authority, scope, owner, review/expiry and minimum evidence; no permanent global hold. Backups are access-restricted and roll off; restored data reapplies deletion/suppression/credential revocation before normal processing. Don't replay old emails/charges after restore. Withdrawal stops marketing dispatch immediately under scope, independent operational obligations remain. Cookie/SDK inventory and consent/withdrawal are based on actual browser/native storage; don't claim all analytics exempt. No exact browsing GPS saved by default. Public page/share metadata excludes private appointments, participant names and hidden addresses.

## Money, consumer commitments and legal documents

Separate SaaS/business subscription, merchant/customer supply, Taplo hardware/domain sale and provider relationships. Merchant direct charges are the current model; actual provider controller/negative balance/fees/cross-border/tax arrangements must match before money use. Cancellation/deposit policy is versioned, visible before acceptance, proportionate and reviewed for scope. A non-refundable label isn't blanket authority. Provider cancellation normally returns applicable unearned prepayment under the assessed agreement/rights; partial fulfilled components need explicit allocation. Unclear statutory/disputed outcome goes to review; no automatic keep-deposit default. Physical goods distance returns/exceptions, services, dated activities, personalised/perishable goods and digital/native supply require their own applicable branches, not one global 14-day rule.

The current price-change policy uses prospective transparent notice/agreement/exit and protects accepted work; no unconditional fee-increase blank cheque or 0%-forever promise. Package/checkout/invoice and legal schedule must match. Tax/VAT model remains an explicit scenario until actual accountant/entity facts; do not carry the disregarded chat arithmetic into billing policy.

## Incidents, complaints, trust and exit

Incident: detect/log time and scope → appoint owner → contain affected capability without destroying evidence → assess data/money/customer impact → determine actual notification duties/deadlines → communicate appropriate facts → recover/reconcile → review root cause. UK GDPR reportable breaches generally require ICO notice without undue delay and within 72 hours of awareness, subject actual applicability/risk assessment; not every technical incident is automatically reportable. Relevant individual notices and processor/controller obligations require actual assessment. Don't wait for perfect information while a genuine deadline runs.

Report → evidence triage → proportionate restriction/notice → reasoned decision → appropriate appeal/reviewer conflict controls. Report count isn't guilt; Native reviews use actual eligibility/authenticity and fair display. Google external review link isn't imported native review or permission to scrape. Fake/unsafe content, impersonation, IP and children/online-safety applicability require assessment and staffed operation. The platform never sells trusted verification merely through a paid plan.

Merchant exit: cancel SaaS renewal separately from domain auto-renew; deliberate downgrade plan; safe accepted-work/refund/reward completion; export authorised records with redaction; disconnect/revoke credentials and staff access; preserve necessary evidence/holds; resolve public links, custom domain routing and NFC destination. Purchased domain remains under actual registrant/transfer terms. Earned rewards don't silently vanish. Customer private transactions remain recoverable under actual exit/retention rights. Physical products require actual supplier/traceability/safety/warranty/returns scope before stock sale.

## Release evidence gates

Gate per feature/category/territory, not broad badge: actual entity and approved publication schedules, actual processor/issuer/scopes/region/SDK disclosures, implemented control, scoped test/rehearsal, responsible operator, professional decision where applicable, monitoring/review trigger. A generated legal summary cannot set verified=true. Unknown prerequisites block the affected production function, while implementation/test fixtures and unrelated safe functions continue. No supplier signup, external message, live transaction or legal approval occurred in the blueprint task.
