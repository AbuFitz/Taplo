## Website/app integration

Main footer: Terms, Privacy, Privacy choices, Reviews policy, Report a business, Help/contact and accessibility route. Owner checkout links merchant software terms and applicable processing/service schedules; customer checkout names the seller and exposes the applicable supply/cancellation terms before commitment. Confirmation retains an immutable accepted agreement and required facts in a durable form. Report/appeal documents are reachable without login/install. Custom-domain mini-spaces name their merchant seller and Taplo's actual role.

Version registry: document ID, scope/audience/locale, version, effective/published dates, approved reviewer/status, source hash, required acceptance event and supersedes link. A privacy notice is presented, not transformed into blanket contractual consent. Marketing choice is separate. Legal updates do not retrospectively replace a booking's accepted policy. Store evidence of applicable version and affirmative choices only where appropriate; do not demand twelve mandatory checkboxes to browse.

Publish gate checks: no unresolved bracket fields; real contacts/entity facts; actual provider/data schedule; approval/scope recorded; linked screens enforce the terms; notices readable/mobile accessible; all reporting/withdrawal/export/cancellation routes function. Include exact outgoing email/SMS templates in legal review when classification matters. No UK GDPR compliant or fully vetted headline solely because these documents exist.

## Current legal research and gaps

ICO's detailed electronic-mail guidance was updated in April 2026, including a charitable soft opt-in; Taplo must not generalise that exception to ordinary merchants. Its April 2026 storage/access guidance includes purpose-specific exceptions with conditions. Adopt consent-first optional tracking until the actual technology fits an assessed exception, rather than claiming every analytics tool now needs no choice. [L1–L2]

CMA review-publisher guidance and Ofcom scope/children materials are incorporated in TRUST-REVIEWS-AND-REPORTING. Merchant-generated business listings plus reviews/messaging need a real Online Safety Act assessment; the provider-content exemption is not assumed. Operational controls, accountable staffing and assessment records are required where applicable, not just wording.

GOV.UK online-selling guidance supports clear pay/total/fulfilment/terms and durable confirmation requirements [L3]. Exact cancellation/early service/digital/date-specific exceptions require category/territory review; a template cannot establish every outcome. Platform tax-reporting scope is a separate accountant/legal workstream: native commerce/personal services may trigger seller information/reporting duties [L4]. Stripe verification does not establish that Taplo's reporting work is complete or performed by Stripe.

Release scope register must cover UK consumer/B2B distinctions, Northern Ireland/product territory differences where relevant, overseas availability/merchants/customers, payment-services/rewards perimeter, prohibited goods, regulated services, accessibility, children/privacy/safety, VAT/platform reporting, domain resale and physical product obligations. No blanket international launch assumed. Do not publish a clinical compliance promise on the basis of a generic tutor/barber workflow.

## Proposed retention construction, not fabricated law

Working operational defaults to assess: unsubmitted drafts 30 days after last meaningful activity; transient technical logs 30 days unless security risk needs a restricted longer window; ordinary resolved support attachments 90 days with a distinct retained case summary; marketing evidence/suppression as necessary to establish and honour the relevant choice. Financial/tax/claims/trust evidence and backups need their own legally reviewed periods and holds. Do not treat these draft service limits as statutory durations or automatically erase records needed for an active dispute.

For each record class, the final schedule identifies purpose/basis, trigger, period/criteria, owner, access, provider propagation, backup expiry, legal hold and deletion/anonymisation evidence. Complete it against real providers before publishing the privacy notice. Data minimisation includes not collecting unsupported sensitive information in the first place.

## Review handoff

Deliver to adviser: these drafts, actual entity/seller map, funds-flow and provider configuration, supported category capabilities, rendered checkout/cancel/report/consent screens, data-role/provider/retention schedules, safety/children/review risk assessments, support rota and incident/rights runbooks. Ask for tracked decisions tied to feature IDs so each legal change becomes a product/command/control requirement. This is more useful than asking someone to bless a footer.
