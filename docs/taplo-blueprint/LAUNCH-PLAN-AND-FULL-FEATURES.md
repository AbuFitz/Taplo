## What the extras mean

UK texts are optional purchased segments: £9 for 100 or £39 for 500. Longer/Unicode messages can use multiple segments; show exact audience/segment cost before send and auto top-up is off. Operational emails are included and do not consume marketing allowance. One campaign to 100 eligible people uses 100 sends. Marketing quota is per account, not multiplied by employee/location; no automatic overage bill.

Core at £12 with no texts remains £12 software. Buy 100 units and the initial software-plus-text purchase is £21; buy 600 units and it is £60. Processing/domain/hardware are separate. Keep visible examples because SMS-heavy operators may prefer another provider’s bundle; no claim Taplo is always cheapest.

Domain connection Core+ means connecting an owned address, not a free domain purchase, business mailbox or custom authenticated email From-domain. Domain buying within Studio stays a separately contracted future path. A public QR/NFC destination is included; its printed/physical accessory is a separate purchase. Rewards are merchant-funded, not Taplo-subsidised.

## Fair limits and exit

Five Free offers means five selectable parent services/products/classes, not every option/variant. Thirty new commitments means unique accepted bookings/reservations or placed orders; edits/reschedules/retries do not recount. Each separately accepted recurring occurrence counts once. Quote/enquiry does not use the allowance until accepted into work; anti-abuse boundaries cannot silently change the promised allowance. Cancelled accepted work still consumed an acceptance count; system duplicates are reversed. Group/mixed fixture reconciliation remains required.

At a limit, preserve existing work/history and necessary communications. Keep drafts and provide next reset/deliberate upgrade. No auto-charge. Downgrades apply under reviewed billing timing, retain accepted obligations and ask owners how to handle excess offers/locations; do not delete work or erase earned rewards. Paid plans have no routine offer/commitment count cap, not unlimited files/abuse/organisations/staffed support.
