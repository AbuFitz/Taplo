# Taplo · Additional category operating profiles

Working edition 2.8 · 3 November 2026. Extends the deeper tutoring/mobile service/retail/workshop profiles in 62 and team map in 99. These are operational specification decisions, not completed specialist legality, exact APIs or final screens. A category label cannot activate an unsupported capability. Mixed businesses combine reviewed capabilities while keeping seller/permission/time/stock rules explicit.

## Barber, hair and beauty

Fields: service name, explicit fixed/from/quote price, total occupied duration, active practitioner segments, any processing interval, buffer, allowed professional(s), chair/room/equipment, location, customer requirements and optional deposit/policy. Processing time is not automatically reusable practitioner time; an owner must configure a supported segment/resource rule. A simple first release may use the conservative total occupancy without claiming advanced processing overlap.

Flow: choose service → named/any eligible professional → location/time → applicable intake → review amount due now/later and cancellation → confirmed or request. Named-worker booking must not silently substitute someone else. Staff-specific prices/durations must be explicit before checkout; no surprise uplift after selection. Add-on services recompute time/resources/total before commitment.

Team: practitioner sees assigned work and minimal customer information, can draft own approved profile/gallery; reception schedules within granted locations; owner manages price/policy, financial rights separately. Contractor/chair renter does not become an independent seller merely by selecting a team role. Absence shows future affected work and requires approved reassignment/customer communication. Departure revokes grants/sessions and deliberately resolves future bookings/public links.

Acceptance: processing overlap conservative fallback; two staff competing for chair; long hair/price-from explanation; allergy/patch-test data only reviewed necessary scope; late arrival/overrun; provider cancellation; named-professional departure; real cancellation/deposit review. No clinical/medical permission inferred.

## Photography and creative projects

Fields: offer/package scope, enquiry vs instant slot, shoot/delivery milestones, resource/travel, deliverables and quantity, revision/change limits, deposit/balance, usage/licence conditions and delivery/access retention. Optional portfolio is deliberately published with actual rights. A private client gallery is not automatically a public sample. Large file delivery and storage need an actual product allowance/cost model.

Flow: view portfolio → brief/request → scoped quote/version → deliberate acceptance/deposit → reserve agreed session/resources → work/approved change → private deliverable release and balance under actual agreement. Accepting a quote doesn't silently allocate a date. Material changes become a new variation, not an overwritten price. Cancelling a shoot and cancelling an entire project are different effects.

Team: photographer assigned project/customer necessities; editor assigned files/deliverables; coordinator dates; finance payment/refunds; client approver and payer can differ. Download/share links require purpose/expiry and access revocation. Support cannot publish gallery to investigate it.

Acceptance: client shares a private link; unauthorised editor; rejected variation; late balance; reshoot/weather postponement; missed deadline; copyright/licence complaint; staff removal; file retention/export and no-longer-available route. Licensing/consumer supply terms require review.

## Fitness, coaching and consulting

Fields: one-to-one/group, actual delivery mode, instructor/venue/equipment, participant rules, credit/package period, attendance, cancellation, any recurring contract and relevant confidential intake. Package credits, recurring payment and membership access are three distinct records. Do not turn all coaches into clinics or collect health information by default.

Flow: choose eligible offer → participant/format → slot/class → credit application or new payment review → attend/deliver → remaining entitlement. A package does not reserve every future slot or create automatic recurring charges. Guardian/minor roles follow the tutoring model where applicable. Online one-to-one, live group and recorded material require separate native-store purchase classification.

Team: assigned coach/consultant gets necessary participants/engagement, attendance and strictly scoped notes; coordinator scheduling; finance balances; no broad sensitive note export from a timetable role. Professional confidentiality/health claims require specialist boundaries.

Acceptance: package exhausted mid-series, expired credits, failed recurring payment without erasing accepted work, instructor substitution, venue full, access to recordings, health-data minimisation, client cancellation and legal/supply branch. Complex memberships remain gated until exact contracts are complete.

## Vehicle stock and viewings

Fields: unique stock identifier, authorised photos, vehicle description/facts, trader identity, public premises/service area, availability, viewing resource/time and enquiry mode. Vehicle reservation/deposit/holding terms and finance-related functionality require separate reviewed capability; a listing is not a binding vehicle-sale checkout.

Flow default: browse unique stock → enquiry or supported viewing → dealer response → agreed next step. Viewing reservation and vehicle reservation are different constraints. One car can have viewing slots without claiming every viewer owns purchase priority. A sold/withdrawn vehicle remains in historic enquiry/appointment records with accurate public status.

Team: stock publisher manages authorised facts/media; sales sees assigned leads/viewings; manager controls broader publication; finance rights separate. No automatic credit broking or licence/vehicle-condition verification claim. Public sources do not prove a dealer owns the car.

Acceptance: concurrent viewing requests vs exclusive vehicle reservation, sold car still in saved link, correction to listing facts after enquiry, independent salesperson, false photos/impersonation report, refund request on unsupported reservation, finance enquiry privacy. Listing/presence can be supported earlier than transactional vehicle sales.

## Food, café and collection

Fields: product/menu, explicit price, relevant required ingredients/allergen/product information with accountable source, options, preparation lead time/capacity, order cut-off, pickup place/time, actual availability and applicable cancellation/returns treatment. Don't reuse barber appointment duration as kitchen capacity. Preparation load and collection queue are distinct constraints.

Flow: choose item/options → fulfilment availability → pickup window → full product/seller/price facts → review/payment → accepted/preparing/ready/collected. A requested time is not confirmed until capacity/merchant acceptance rules permit. Food safety/product rules need specialist review; generic change-of-mind returns cannot simply be applied without scope assessment.

Team: prep gets relevant lines/instructions; counter collection code/order context; menu fact publisher controls material changes; finance refund separately. Never silently substitute an allergen-relevant item or rely on a customer free-text note as a complete safety process.

Acceptance: kitchen capacity race, cutoff/timezone closure, sold-out ingredient, unsafe substitution, changed allergen facts, late/no collection, merchant cancellation and refund, public review/report. No automatic universal food support from category search.

## Health/clinic scheduling boundary

Default scope is restricted reviewed scheduling/presence, not a clinical record/diagnosis/prescription system. Fields: practitioner/service identity, eligible public claims, location/delivery, appointment duration and necessary contact/booking facts. Professional qualifications/permissions and sensitive intake require explicit review before enabling that branch.

Flow: public service information → eligible booking/request → minimal necessary contact/review → private scheduling information. Avoid sensitive treatment/diagnostic detail in public titles, link previews, pass fronts, emails/subjects or push. A customer relationship doesn't automatically prove patient/guardian authority.

Team: assigned scheduling access only, receptionist minimum facts, practitioner restricted access when reviewed; audit reads/exports appropriately. Retention/legal hold, urgent care guidance and professional confidentiality cannot be copied from retail returns.

Acceptance: wrong patient/guardian, shared device, practitioner departure, confidential appointment label, deletion request vs retention, misleading claims, emergency enquiry and scope refusal. Block unsupported clinical workflows while leaving genuinely safe unrelated page tools available.

## Category search, adaptation and release

Search understands colloquial aliases and explains recommended capabilities, e.g. detailer → car detailing/mobile services. Keep other/mixed and manual choice. Matching may suggest but cannot fabricate legal status, qualify professional claims or grant permissions. Icons/labels adapt by task while Settings/Payments meanings remain stable. Before operational release for any profile, bind each field/action to exact records/errors/screens/permissions/evidence; this document deepens semantics and does not declare equivalent finished implementation for all categories.
