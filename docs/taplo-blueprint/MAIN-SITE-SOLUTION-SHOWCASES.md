# Taplo · Solution showcases on the main website

Working edition 2.5 · 3 November 2026. Founder clarification: business-type pages sell Taplo's solution on the main marketing website. They do not live inside customer Explore or represent individual merchants. Proposed routes, not a live deployment. Extends spec 105 without changing the customer's block-first business experience.

## Three different kinds of page

| Kind | Example | Main action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Taplo marketing solution | mytaplo.com/solutions/barbers | Understand the fit, see an example, start a draft |
| Customer discovery | mytaplo.com/explore with filters | Find an actual eligible business and act |
| Merchant mini-space | mytaplo.com/<handle> or merchant host | Book, buy, enquire and return to that business |

Never call the first an owner profile/page in navigation. Marketing navigation uses Solutions, Features, Examples, Pricing and Help, plus a clear Explore link for customers and Sign in. Solutions may group by business type and by outcome. Main-site marketing content can introduce the customer app, but it does not insert software sales pitches into every customer booking task.

## One high-quality showcase recipe

1. Hero names the actual work: **Your work. Your bookings. Your regulars.** Support: **Give your barber business a page worth sharing, a clear way to book, and an easy way for customers to come back.** Actions: Create your page / See the experience.
2. Show a truthful compact business page with product/service rail, not a generic laptop collage. A labelled illustrative merchant can demonstrate the block popup, booking and staff selector. Keep the illustration distinct from real testimonials.
3. Explain the connected customer journey: share link/NFC → choose service/person → pay eligible deposit → get reminder → return/manage. Visible examples, short text and optional user-controlled motion; no endless autoplay/video sequence.
4. Show Studio doing the work: day calendar, staff scope, in-context setup and one actual useful outcome. Mobile composition must demonstrate the phone workflow, not squeeze the desktop screenshot into a tiny device frame.
5. Show category-specific capabilities and honest scope. Barber: time/chair/person/buffer, team profile links and optional deposits. Tutor: payer/learner, timetable and supported series. Mobile service: service area/intake/quote/travel. Retail: variants/stock/fulfilment. Workshop: dated capacity/participants/waitlist.
6. Explain existing-website coexistence with a specific task-link example. No implied Shopify stock/order synchronisation.
7. Show relevant package fit from the canonical catalogue; no invented category surcharge. Essential safety/team access remains included; optional texts and provider fees clearly linked.
8. Answer genuine task questions and link relevant feature/guide pages. Finish with one clear Create your page action retaining category context without forcing setup choices.

The main marketing site may be more editorial and expressive than a merchant mini-space. This does not make merchant pages conventional websites. Use the same type/icon/token system, strong neutral contrast and useful demonstrations. Do not repeat the logo/name, add fake dashboard numbers or invent owner testimonials. A category selector changes examples, not plan authority or legal eligibility.

## SEO construction

Each showcase owns a distinct useful intent and URL/title/heading. Initial title pattern: Taplo for barbers | Booking, business pages and returning customers. The final title follows actual available features and search evidence, not an arbitrary keyword count. Use a concise genuine description, crawlable internal links, useful server-rendered content, canonical and eligible public share image from spec 106. Structured data reflects the page's real subject; do not inject merchant rating data into a software-solution page.

Research query intent and current search results before large-scale content production. No measured keyword volume or ranking forecast exists yet. Publish a smaller set of strong distinct solutions rather than every profession × town. Customer local collections require actual supply and their own index policy. Google spam guidance warns against doorway/scaled low-value content; reusable engineering does not justify near-identical articles.

Marketing publishing contract: content author drafts; category/product reviewer checks exact capability; legal/compliance reviewer checks regulated/price/claims; authorised publisher schedules a version. Retire/update content when capabilities or prices change. Preview/social cards and search metadata use that same published version. Consent-required advertising/analytics stay gated; sign-up conversion metrics cannot justify tracking private customer activity.
