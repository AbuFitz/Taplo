## Change record template

For a meaningful change, record: previous assumption; new decision; evidence; affected journeys, records, permissions and documentation; transaction/migration impact; owner; date; validation and rollback. Supersede old decisions rather than quietly deleting the reasoning.

Small design experiments need no elaborate approval ceremony. Changes to money, legal promises, permissions or existing customer commitments need corresponding validation. Nothing is locked forever; existing agreements still have to be honoured or changed through an appropriate process.

## Evidence labels

- Observed: seen in the current read-only site review or inspected source, with scope stated.
- Documented competitor capability: first-party documentation, not an independent usability result.
- Recommendation: our proposed product behaviour.
- Hypothesis: usefulness/differentiation requiring customer validation.
- Open: missing business decision, research or specialist review.

Do not turn a recommendation into a factual claim that the feature already works. Do not turn an internal implementation limitation into permanent public-facing copy.
