## Operating readiness and legal schedules

Create an actual schedule record for: contracting entity/contact; launch countries/currency; merchant/consumer relationship; payment controller/loss/refund authority; privacy roles/purposes; subprocessors/regions/transfers; retention/legal holds; cookies/SDKs; children/specialist categories; review/content safety/report/appeal; physical products/domains; store disclosures; complaints and insurance applicability. Each has accountable real owner, legal/professional question if needed, evidence source/version/date, implementation controls and review trigger. Placeholder fields stay blocked. No broad claim that Stripe handles all Taplo duties.

Rehearsals: lost owner phone, removed staff with accepted work, customer deletion request, incorrect public address, impersonating merchant, refund unknown result, message abuse, business closure with active rewards, compromised API secret, provider outage and restore. Produce a case timeline and ownership/actions; do not execute a destructive production drill. Safety/financial/access assistance works for Free too.

Support launch policy: help and email/in-product case route with actual staffed hours, an owner and escalation backup. Set response targets only from real capacity. Automated triage/answers clearly expose unresolved state and human access; no robot promising final deposit/legal decisions. Console uses explicit action and approval scopes, not full customer impersonation by default. Taplo/platform and merchant/customer disputes have separate owners.

## Customer observation tasks prepared

Recruit independently authorised participants later; don't infer that user instruction here authorises messages. Use fictional/test businesses and reversible tasks; tell participants the study purpose, collect appropriate consent and minimise recordings/retention. Staff don't coach unless the task is blocked; log intervention.

| Participant/task | Observe | Decision signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New owner: recognise draft, create first useful offer, add hours/stock, understand publish | Lost context, invented facts, re-entry, expected meaning, correctness not clicks | Can explain what is public/ready and what remains; unsafe misunderstandings are defects |
| Team owner/member: invite, understand access, see assigned work, leave/revoke | Overbroad visibility, unclear role summary, shared login temptation | Correct role/field understanding and no cross-scope disclosure |
| Customer: find business from direct link and Explore, book/order with deposit or none | Seller/price/confirmation understanding, drop-off and accessibility | Predicts correct amount/status before commitment; finds return record without forced install |
| Returning customer: manage accepted work/reward, decline marketing | Correct private access, familiar business identity, unsubscribe vs operational difference | No guessed account ownership or hidden opt-in; useful return without Explore |
| Merchant: choose package and optional messages | Bill comprehension, perceived value, forced-feature concerns | Actual commercial choice/explanation, not polite agreement with a leading question |
| Operations: recover unknown payment/message/domain result | Case handoff, duplicate work, authority and time | Finds authoritative result and permitted action without repeat charge or privacy shortcut |

Start with a small diverse diagnostic round (for example 5–8 owners plus 5–8 customers), include mobile/desktop and relevant accessibility needs; sample size is a planning choice, not statistical market validation. Fix material failures and rerun affected tasks. No invented conversion percentage or user quote. Time/ratings alone don't prove willingness to pay.

## Commercial/cost observation

Offer truthful plan choices and optional fee alternatives using consistent actual scope/total examples; avoid leading framing. Record selected plan, abandoned choice/reason, existing tools/workflow, expected messages, paid captured volume, actual retained use and support/usage cost. A pricing survey is weaker than deliberate real paid choice and retention under an authorised pilot. Do not charge/store real payment details within this blueprint study plan.

Measure activation from a genuinely usable published offer, completed customer action and owner handling—not signups alone. Track 30/60/90-day task/retention cohorts when real launch exists. Separate attributed acquisition from repeat work and suppressed marketing. Actual outcomes will update assumptions; current margin/GMV/support figures remain hypotheses.

## Evidence state and automation authority

Each work packet: ID, prerequisites, accountable actual owner, selected version/scope, fixture, expected result, observed result, source artefact, defect, decision and retest. Defaults are specified or blocked_prerequisite, never passed. Agents may assemble/check artefacts and propose fixes, but cannot create evidence of an unperformed real action or sign legal approval. An unknown requirement is escalated as a scoped question; it doesn't become a fatal generic warning across unrelated tasks.
