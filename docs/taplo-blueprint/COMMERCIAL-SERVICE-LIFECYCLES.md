# Pricing and operational responsibilities by service

Edition 0.6 · Commercial architecture, not approved prices, allowances or margin. The product catalogue is broader than a single subscription tier table.

| Service | Cost and responsibility to model | Customer-facing terms needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio subscription | Hosting, data/storage, support, feature access and operating margin | Billing period, trial if offered, renewal, downgrade/export and wind-down |
| Messaging | Channel/provider delivery cost, retries and support | Included usage, overage/top-up if chosen, failed-delivery treatment |
| Payments | Chosen charge model, processor fees, disputes, refunds and negative balance exposure | Merchant responsibilities, displayed fees, payout and refund treatment |
| Domain registration | Term-specific registrar price, renewal/redemption costs, payment/provider failure and support | Exact initial/renewal quote, auto-renew, registrant rights, transfer-out and separate SaaS cancellation |
| Domain connection | Hosting verification/certificate operations and support | What is included; bring-your-own does not imply registration/email service |
| NFC hardware | Chip/inlay, enclosure, printing, encoding/quality control, packaging, shipping, replacements and support | Compatibility, claim, physical return/replacement policy, destination changes and lost-card treatment |
| Wallet passes | Issuer/signing credentials, issuance/update operations, support and optional redemption infrastructure | Eligible pass type, lifespan, merchant programme rules and update behaviour |
| Native apps | Store/distribution, native bridge maintenance, device testing, privacy and support | Supported OS/device scope, feature availability and account/installation behaviour |
| Rewards | Merchant incentive cost/liability, fraud and refund/reversal support | Earning/redemption/expiry and closure terms; platform-wide settlement is separate scope |
| Custom domains plus NFC | Stable platform redirect uptime, preferred-host changes and fallback | What continues if a domain expires or an owner leaves hosting |

## Recommended packaging principles

Keep routine use easy to understand. Treat recurring SaaS fees, annual domain registration, one-off hardware and metered operating costs as distinct charge types even if a package bundles them. A “free domain for one year” promotion would still need an eligible-TLD cap, renewal price, ownership/transfer rules and treatment when the subscription ends. Do not introduce that promise before the economics and provider arrangement are agreed.

A domain price shown in search is not an indefinitely reserved quote. Show current availability, term, currency/tax treatment, initial price and renewal context. A registrar premium name must not accidentally consume an ordinary included allowance. Refund and cancellation policy must follow actual provider/TLD/transaction rules rather than a universal subscription refund assumption.

Keep entitlements separate from data ownership. A downgraded business should retain permitted access/export and appropriate customer transaction records; domain registration and accessories should not be silently confiscated. Define the ongoing redirect/hosting service promise for physical accessories explicitly, including business closure and platform wind-down. Avoid uncosted lifetime guarantees.

Before setting plans, run worked examples for solo services, a small shop plus workshops and a growing team: provider costs, message/pass volume, support time, refunds/disputes, acquisition and margin. Pricing can change with the evidence; existing commitments need an appropriate change process.
