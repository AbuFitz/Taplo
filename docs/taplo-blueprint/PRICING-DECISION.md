# Taplo · The package decision

Working edition 2.3 · 3 November 2026 · Current recommended launch model, not published charges or validated demand. This decision supersedes the included-SMS package recommendation in 108 and the earlier single-paid-plan rollout. All four packages are in the launch plan. Rationale and assumptions are explicit; do not change prices again without a material input, cost, scope or demand finding.

## The decision in one sentence

Sell four useful software packages, include routine operational email and team access, and offer one optional SMS service with a clear price and spending control. Do not make everyone pay for a small SMS bundle they may not need.

## Simple communication policy

Operational email is part of the service, not a charge for each booking confirmation. Marketing email is explicitly bounded above; at the limit, pause new campaigns until reset or deliberate upgrade. No launch-time marketing email overage bill. Operational messages are not stopped to sell an upgrade.

SMS is the single optional metered communication service: 100 UK SMS units for £9, or 500 for £39 as a larger pack of the same service. No recurring SMS subscription required. Unit means a provider-billed segment; the composer says This uses 1 text / 2 texts, and shows total audience cost. Purchased units persist under reviewed account/exit terms, while receipts show exact purchase. No automatic top-up by default; optional auto top-up requires threshold, amount, monthly cap and appropriate spending permission.

Avoid a complicated separate wallet for SMS, reminders, marketing, staff texts and every provider. One balance and one use history with internal purpose/consent/rate distinctions. Only supported UK SMS is covered by these packs; international/WhatsApp is not quietly charged at another rate. Don't add more communication products to the launch bill until their value, supplier rights and economics are clear.

If SMS is not funded or cannot be delivered, show the true state and use permitted email/in-app alternatives where available. Never promise successful reminder delivery, send on another marketing channel after opt-out, or charge again because a job was retried. A one-way sender cannot advertise SMS replies.

## Why return to £12/£24/£49?

This is a package change supported by the same model, not a new unexplained price guess. Removing the shared SMS subsidy lowers cost while retaining the everyday software. At unchanged support/infrastructure/email assumptions, direct contribution is £5.85/£11.49/£22.92 per paid account, versus £4.60/£7.74/£15.42 when those same prices included 25/75/150 texts. Users who want SMS pay for their actual chosen units. No SMS-pack income is assumed in the base viability case.

The £15/£29/£59 included-SMS alternative remains in the comparison file. It can generate more contribution, but its higher base and small allowance are not automatically a clearer or more attractive offer. The current recommended decision prioritises a complete usable software price plus one obvious optional service. No assertion that every audience prefers it or that the whole market will pay.

Booksy's retrieved UK price is £40 plus £5 per extra user excluding VAT, with a substantial included messaging/feature bundle (R73). In a standard-20%-VAT comparison, software is £48 for solo or £72 for five users. This does not make feature sets identical. The busy-solo/five-user SMS examples can favour Booksy; do not hide that or claim Taplo is cheapest. Taplo must justify its compact identity/native commerce/website coexistence/customer-return experience, and select a supplier/channel model appropriate to real use.

## What must be true for profitability

Base estimates retain 5/10/20 support minutes per paid account/month at £0.50/min, four active Free accounts per paid, £0.45 per active Free account, a 60/30/10 paid mix, current modelled VAT/payment fees and explicit shared budgets. These are assumptions/operating budgets, not actual usage. With no included SMS and no assumed SMS profit, 1,000 paid plus 4,000 active Free leaves about £1,948 after the stated serving, Free, shared, replacement acquisition/onboarding and £3,000 engineering allowance, before founder pay, other unbudgeted overhead and tax. At 500 paid that same model does not yet fund the £3,000 engineering allowance.

So this is a plausible lean model, not a guaranteed profitable company at any size. A cheap cloud invoice cannot fund unlimited support or acquisition. Founder labour is costed and cannot be erased to make the plan look viable. See ECONOMICS-RESULTS for all price/bundle/stress scenarios and exact exclusions.

## Operating budgets and review triggers

| Indicator | Initial model/review threshold | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct paid contribution | Aim at least 55% of net subscription revenue in the model | Review provider, usage, workflow/support root cause and package fit; not a guarantee or industry benchmark |
| Mean support minutes | 5 Core / 10 Pro / 20 Business monthly, onboarding separate | Investigate confusing setup/repeated incidents; no customer stopwatch or refusal of essential help |
| Active Free subsidy | Model £0.45/account and four Free per paid | Measure cohorts; improve conversion/abuse/serving efficiency without deleting legitimate work |
| Acquisition plus onboarding | Model £30 + 20 minutes (£10) | Measure actual cohort payback/retention; spend cap before scaling |
| Monthly paid churn | Model 5%, not a target or external benchmark | Investigate reasons; don't compensate by unbounded paid acquisition |
| SMS supplier cost | Model 5p/segment with exact sender/FX/fee review | Reprice optional service or negotiate only on actual agreement; no silent overage |
| Cloud/assets/search | Assumed per-account variable allocations and stated fixed pool | Usage alerts, tenant-safe caching, image optimisation, bounded rendering/search; no unmeasured scale claim |

Track by relevant cohorts and usage, not only averages that conceal heavy loss-making accounts. Normal operational variation is expected; review must not automatically raise prices or change old agreements. Communicate any necessary future commercial change transparently under actual terms.

## Launch logistics

All four packages appear from launch with exact supported features, entitlement checks, invoices/tax, Free metering, upgrade/downgrade, cancellation/grace, refunds/closure and team rules. Use one current entitlement matrix and one billing catalogue. A feature cannot be locked in UI but callable through another route, or advertised in Pro while unimplemented. A billing failure cannot strand customers' accepted commitments.

Provide a truthful plan finder using capabilities/locations and optional SMS volume, showing actual expected cash purchases and carry-forward. Never automatically suggest Business purely because the merchant has five people or hide a cheaper suitable package. Show Your plan £24/month; optional SMS only if you buy it—not a mysterious estimated total with many compulsory extras.
