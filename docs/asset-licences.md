## Rules this implies for the design engine
- The gallery and setup must only show assets from rows marked Recorded.
- Illustration assets are shown as illustration; they are never presented as a customer's own work.
- AGPL-licensed image libraries and tools are not suitable for the product; none were added in this pass.
- No background-removal or other destructive image processing is performed on an owner's upload; cropping and focal point are stored as settings on the image reference.

## Open items (owner decisions)
1. The delivered marketing films (`public/marketing/film`, `reveal`) contain adapted CC BY-SA 4.0 vehicle photographs. They are published with attribution and a CC BY-SA statement (their `CREDITS.md`, `/credits`), which meets the licence. Whether Taplo wants its films under CC BY-SA, or re-rendered without the cars, is a decision for the owner.
2. Real photographs of people (Unsplash barber and engineer) are used; model-release status is Unsplash's, not verified here.
