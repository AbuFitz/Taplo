# Visual reusable looks and styles — 24 October 2026

The owner design shelf now shows real rendered previews of saved page designs, using the current business name, photographs, offerings and sections. It replaces the earlier name-only chips. Applying a saved look still copies only its theme and page overrides; content, section order/IDs and each section's own appearance remain intact. The active design is identified visually and with aria-pressed. Removing a saved design leaves its currently applied appearance in place; undo can restore the saved entry.

Saved section styles also have real previews. Each card shows its appearance applied to the current section and its existing records. Selecting a style copies it onto the selected section; the separate All control retains the existing operation on sections of the same kind. Editing an applied copy does not mutate its saved source. The snapshot is styling, not a second copy of business content.

Decorative preview routes validate the selected saved IDs against the current page document, copy appearance and use the existing PageRenderer. They do not write to storage, import demonstration records or replace a published document. Arbitrary URLs/CSS are not accepted as look IDs. Preview motion is disabled. An IntersectionObserver mounts visible shelf previews and releases off-screen frames so full shelves do not run every decorative page concurrently; controls remain keyboard accessible and the frames are inert.

The existing schema limits remain 12 page looks and 20 section styles. Saving at capacity now explains that an entry must be removed, rather than silently evicting the oldest saved design. Runtime mutation guards and the disabled save controls agree. Existing saved documents and legacy appearance settings remain compatible.

The public appearance workbench now opens on the current Station Road example. Its business selector is readable with the expanded industry catalogue. Changing a look sends an appearance update to the current frame rather than navigating it; existing customer content can stay open while comparing designs. Switching business remains explicit. Its saved page-design cards use a palette/layout miniature because workbench looks are transient rather than owner storage; owner cards use the real saved-document preview.

Verification covers saved source isolation, unknown-ID fallback, preserving widget settings, current owner content, apply/delete/undo/redo/reload at 390 and 1440 pixels, capacity protection and an unchanged preview document/open FAQ across look changes. This does not close the entire engine matrix or replace physical-device/keyboard/performance verification of unrelated flows.
