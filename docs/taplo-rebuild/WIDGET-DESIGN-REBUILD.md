# Widget design before marketing motion

24 October 2026. The owner's direct correction supersedes the broad scheduler prompt: improve actual widgets and their editing choices first; only then rebuild the marketing reveal. Marketing artwork may be original simplified illustrative UI, rather than literal renderer screenshots. Never copy the reference creator's portrait, logo, headphones or colour treatment.

Taplo helps local businesses turn visits into calls, enquiries, bookings and useful information. A widget must have a recognizable purpose and a deliberate silhouette, density and action. Changing only a global radius/shadow is insufficient.

Design approach: one material vocabulary, multiple content structures. Individual service tiles, editorial lead offers, grouped menu panels, compact status/hours, clear visit cards, typographic notes and separate expandable answers. One level of floating surface per collection, consistent readable prices and durations, touch targets at least 44px, quiet maintained icons and restrained motion. White, paper and ink remain the default; owner colours and readability repair still work. The owner's records remain the only content source for the actual product.

First implementation includes service/menu structure, hours/status, visit, review, text and FAQ variants, their schema-backed visual choices, and example compositions. Preserve stored defaults and existing paths. Do not fabricate reviews or ratings: Google cards remain review links until import exists. Preserve booking/detail/enquiry contexts and honest browser-only outcomes.

Then build an original marketing composition: simple service/price, slot, identity and location shapes with minimal copy and clear hierarchy, staged orbital motion and real useful destination. It is illustrative product artwork, not a fake booking, rating or metric. Code-native components use the established icon system, not generated bitmap icons. No new runtime library. Respect reduced motion and off-screen budgets.

Acceptance: actual shape differences at 320/390/768/1440, no nested redundant panels, clear price hierarchy and wrapping, record selection unchanged, safe existing-page defaults, working keyboard/detail/booking handoff, reduced motion and accessibility. Verify real editor choices with owner data and actual customer renderer before marketing animation. Remaining widgets/profile/portal/theme/media work stays explicit; a completed test suite is not design completion.
