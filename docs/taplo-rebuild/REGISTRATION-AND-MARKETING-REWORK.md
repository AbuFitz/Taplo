# Registration, marketing and logo-aware backgrounds

24 October 2026. Owner’s latest correction supersedes the previous visual checkpoint. Main priority is a substantive mobile setup and marketing rework; full legal pages are secondary and still need verified operator/contact details.

## Design read
People should configure a useful business page in a short, coherent mobile workspace, seeing their own page respond immediately. Replace the long document with a six-step viewport: identity, business type, action/contact, first content, layout, review. The actual page remains visible and expands on demand. Inputs stay legible at16px; VisualViewport resizing keeps the workspace above a software keyboard without disabling zoom. Desktop uses the same state and choices with an expanded live canvas. Existing seven-step drafts mapping to goal resume at the merged action/contact decision.

Icons use maintained Lucide SVGs (ISC),22px with2px strokes and visible business labels. No generated icon assets or new dependency. Large vacant icon tiles become compact choices. Loading reflects actual hydration with a restrained top line/skeleton, never an artificial delay.

Marketing removes nested homepage iframes, fake device bezels, rotated/cropped feature canvases and repeated layout-name gallery headings. The gallery leads with business names and frameless actual captures. Feature storytelling uses authored HTML/SVG-library illustrations of prices, booking times, stock and team selection. A white canvas and slow monochrome atmosphere alternate with the dark site. Main film is silent autoplay, inline and looping while visible, with no native controls or “Read the film” panel; OS reduced-motion uses a poster.

## Logo-aware gradient contract
Silver and Smoke are neutral motion layouts. Optional gradient source=logo follows sampled identity colour metadata; transparent backgrounds are excluded from sampling. Monochrome logos have no invented hue. Light/dark tonal ranges keep body copy readable; the existing resolver still repairs contrast. A changed upload changes a linked gradient. Explicit accent overrides survive; manually editing gradient endpoints detaches the link. Identity pixels and proportions are never recoloured. Logo metadata is optional so previous content remains valid.

## Real photography and scope
Primary examples replace synthetic barber, detailing, café and vehicle photography with licensed real images. Synthetic named staff/tutor portraits and the generated styling-product image are removed. Photographs illustrate layouts and do not claim endorsement or that photographed people work for the example business. Vehicle photos are representative models; names/year/fuel are aligned to the pictured models. Public /credits carries authors, sources, licences and adaptation notes; stock pages link to it. CC BY-SA image derivatives retain that licence. Price/mileage/availability remain the authored isolated example data, not factual sale listings.

Existing stored owner looks remain available. Backend authentication/public publishing/payments/notifications/Google imports remain later work. No account or payment is simulated as connected. Google-only reviews remain unchanged.
