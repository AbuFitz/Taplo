# Business compositions — 24 October 2026

## Design read
Taplo helps local service customers see the work, understand prices and take the correct next action on a phone. The six lead examples should demonstrate that task rather than advertise a colour preset. Use neutral surfaces, existing original business imagery, clear service prices and selectively grouped sections. Desktop must retain the same content and transaction semantics, with authored wider layouts. Saved owners retain their previous looks.

## Implemented
- Jay’s Barbers: white photo profile with a short fade, then an inverted cuts/prices section; booking, barber selection and counter products remain available.
- Detail Dept.: genuine neutral black, editorial work photography and open service descriptions; appointment requests remain subject to agreement.
- Harris Cars: white editorial introduction and a wider stock catalogue; available/reserved/sold and selected-car enquiry context are unchanged.
- No. 6 Coffee: open menu on white, with quiet grouped opening times. Menu items never start appointments.
- Dan’s Home Repairs: photograph and introduction followed by open services and a grouped service area; scope and from/quote pricing remain explicit.
- Maths with Aisha: personal portrait, a grouped introduction and open paid-session list; section spacing separates the journey on mobile.

All section treatments use the same editable recipes and renderer as the owner workspace. Starting from an example copies presentation, but strips record references and sample share names. Appearance objects are cloned, so editing a new owner’s page cannot mutate the example. The six defaults and five wordmarks are neutral; older stored look IDs remain resolvable and selectable in the full editor.

Homepage layout discovery now leads with Studio, Service, Personal and Midnight. The main chooser image has no nested browser/device border. Refreshed 25 real-render homepage/gallery captures and four neutral look thumbnails. Names and prices are the existing illustrative UK fixture content; no new proof, ratings or credentials were fabricated.
