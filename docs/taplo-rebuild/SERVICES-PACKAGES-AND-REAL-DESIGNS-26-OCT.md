# Services, real designs and owner follow-ups, 26 October

## Shared engine

Services now have an optional ordered “What’s included” list. Owners can add, reorder and remove up to eight items, with blank/length validation, keyboard focus and announced changes. The same record feeds featured blocks, spotlight cards, detail popups and the booking model. Featured services can display their photo and open the shared service detail; only three inclusions appear in the block, with the full list in the detail.

A booking block keeps its selected service when promoted into the profile. External service destinations remain direct website links, including on plans without Taplo booking. All-website choices offer website destinations without an unrelated enquiry form; mixed choices keep website destinations separate from local enquiry choices. Missing/archived/plan-limited selections do not silently become a different service. Hover colours are checked against actual button text. Three-photo and bounded gallery media fill their available width rather than shrinking sideways when height is capped.
