# Business design discovery

24 October2026. Audience: a local business owner comparing useful page structures on their phone. Primary journey: find a relevant example, try the real demo, start with its arrangement and section types, then replace the sample with their own records. The live renderer remains the source of truth. This is implementation pending owner review, not a claim that the whole engine is finished.

The owner rejected a white library with examples fading into it, plus framed individual artwork. The library now uses a pointer-responsive moving dark neutral field. Actual page images have deliberate breathing room without an enclosing coloured preview card. Two readable desktop columns replace tiny four-column examples. All phone previews use the same viewport proportion; desktop previews consistently show the actual desktop capture. Search matches business name, sector and real capabilities, including accent-normalized café/cafe. Existing style/capability/industry filters compose with search. Each design links directly to its usable demo as well as its own setup context. The listing contains no interactive iframe or wheel trap.

Eight examples are discoverable. Two existing underused sectors are deliberately recomposed: Hollin Physiotherapy has a white editorial introduction, open appointment rows and softly grouped team with split desktop structure. Forge Strength has a photographic wide introduction, true-black composition, a white introductory service spotlight and class/coaching rows. They use the actual owner controls and section treatments; there is no special demo-only renderer. Synthetic testimonials and accreditation labels are removed from these newly surfaced examples. Existing licensed studio photographs and retained vector logos are used; no generated people or icons added. Clinic appointments are requests; the studio intro is an honest browser-only booking demonstration.

Starting with an example carries its look and relevant widget structure while scrubbing sample service/record references. The sample's business name, prices and media never become the owner's records. Existing-owner look application preserves their content and section arrangement. The larger guided replacement of every relevant team/gallery/stock record remains OPEN, along with more sector compositions and deeper engine/profile choices.

The live detail preview keeps one iframe document across phone/desktop switching, preserving booking and typed details. A390px maximum phone viewport and an80px fixed-navigation inset avoid misleading scale and covered controls. Native review caught smooth parent scrolling and step-heading focus moving the embedded frame; the shared controller now repositions immediately only when needed and booking focus does not scroll the parent document. Only the booking popup's own body returns to the start of a new step. Unscaled previews omit an identity transform.

See CONNECTED-PRESENTATION.md for the related mobile setup, sharing, plaque, hero and latest owner corrections. No physical-device/backend readiness or design approval is implied. Actual production build, lint/unit, rendered image, full-flow, native-engine and public release evidence belongs in the handoff.
