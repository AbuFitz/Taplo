# Visual reset — latest owner direction, 24 October

The owner rejected the overnight visual direction. Earlier warm cream, paper, olive and safe card compositions are not an approved baseline. Passing checks and completed engineering do not satisfy this design brief.

## Current direction

Professional, modern, minimal and visually expressive. Taplo keeps its own black, white, cobalt and cyan identity. Do not copy Linktree's palette, branding, wording or exact layouts. Use the supplied Linktree screenshots as evidence of interaction quality: one decision per screen; tactile visual tiles; recognisable platform marks; a visible live page while choosing a theme; compact editing sheets; a meaningful result after each action. The owner explicitly prefers more visual interaction and less reading.

Apple references concern authored product presentation: multiple deliberate camera angles, lighting, materials, close-ups, depth, feature-specific compositions, carefully paced motion and isolated useful UI. A slideshow of screenshots is not a finished marketing film. Device renders must show Taplo's actual renderer and honest functionality.

## Product requirements added this morning

- Google reviews only. No internal reviews, self-entered testimonials or manually invented ratings. Existing stored records must not be destroyed, but they must not appear as Google content. Until Google data is connected, use a clear Google destination and explain the actual connection state in owner setup.
- Every widget needs its own researched setup and customer presentation. Booking, prices, stock, Google reviews, social links, profile pictures and page sections need considered design, not just a global colour swap.
- Mobile and desktop both require deliberate compositions and visual review.
- Starting examples should feel like credible UK businesses with confident modern colour, current imagery and useful real offerings.
- Marketing should isolate and demonstrate each capability rather than repeatedly placing full phone screenshots in generic cards.

## Copy and example identity correction
The owner rejected “Different work. Distinct design.”, “Your business. A page that feels like it.”, prominent “fictional UK businesses” notices, and the generic place-name examples. Public copy must describe a concrete use or customer action. Lead design examples now use Jay’s Barbers, Detail Dept., Harris Cars, No. 6 Coffee, Dan’s Home Repairs and Maths with Aisha. These are editorial design examples, never customer endorsements. Keep internal record IDs stable. Coordinate wordmarks, palette, services, staff, prices, photos and captures. Harris Cars uses a light, cobalt-accented stock layout instead of purple. No invented Google reviews or ratings. Production connections remain explicitly documented; removing showcase disclaimers does not turn browser-only actions into live services.


## Superseding owner direction: monochrome platform and product-led homepage
The owner rejected the cobalt/cyan homepage, the repeated headline-and-banner sections, and the incorrectly proportioned hero handset. Taplo’s platform website and setup UI must be black, white and neutral greys. Customer design examples retain their own brand colours. Reviewed the current Framer homepage and its compact grouped platform menu, plus OneSimpleSite’s indexed full-screen navigation. Apply restrained typography, real feature-specific UI presentations, smaller correct device proportions and a more varied page rhythm. The handset must contain an actual 390×844 viewport, scaled proportionally, including after responsive changes. Do not deploy the rejected version. Existing build and test evidence predates this further redesign and must be refreshed.
