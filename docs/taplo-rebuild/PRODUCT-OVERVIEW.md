24 October 2026. Scope: /product only. Keep the released mobile setup and Booking page intact.

Promise: a local business page that helps a customer take the next useful step. Audience: owners who need to see their own kind of work represented before starting.

Problem: ten repeated artwork cards obscure the product model, overuse the barber and repeat individual feature pages. Replace with one business chooser, an editorial menu composition, a vehicle-specific stock composition and a compact complete feature directory.

Creative thesis: the same engine has different jobs. Clinic, café, trades and forecourt examples replace a generic app montage. Real renderer captures, shared handset geometry and open whitespace carry the proof. The chooser updates the actual example destination and adoption link together. No fake live activity or fabricated customer results.

No autoplay carousel, scroll takeover or new library. Mobile reads heading, choices, phone, action. Desktop pairs copy and handset. Reduced motion stops the stage and entrance movement. The complete feature directory stays server rendered.

Verified: existing fictional example content and capture manifest; real demos/adoption routes; current feature routes. Provisional: marketing composition, subject to owner review. Live backend features remain later; the page links to contextual feature disclosures and previews without claiming real bookings or messaging.

Done means: 320/390/768/1440 widths inspected; all four selections update name/demo/adoption without moving the page; keyboard and reduced-motion checks; complete feature navigation; accessibility scan; no horizontal overflow or browser errors; production build.
